Animal Flow is a ground based bodyweight system. You learn a handful of foundational shapes — Beast, Crab, Limb Lifts, Underswitch — and then sequence them into flows that look like dance and feel like training.

- Builds joint resilience for hips, shoulders, wrists, spine
- Develops coordination, balance, and proprioception
- No equipment. No mirrors. Just you, the floor, and the music
- Scales from total beginner to certified instructor
