## Why this matters

Bone density. Fall prevention. Maintaining the strength to live the life you want at 70, 80, 90. The data is overwhelming: lifting weights in your 50s, 60s and beyond is one of the highest-impact things you can do for long term health.

## What to look for in a class

Small groups. A coach who actually watches your technique. Real lifts, scaled to where your body is today.
