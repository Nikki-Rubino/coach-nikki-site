Hey,

Well, it turns out the universe decided to test my balance, and this week's surprise is an eviction notice. Who knew life was so committed to keeping me on my toes?

Now, you might be wondering: what's the correlation between an eviction and training? Buckle up, because here's the scoop. Training isn't just about lifting heavy and breaking a sweat; it's a crash course in life's unpredictability. The art of training teaches us the beauty of repetition, the sweet melody of resilience, the art of patience, and the courage to keep going even when the universe decides to play its version of chess with your life. Simply one rep after another until the desired task is successful.

Now, I'm no stranger to challenges. This whole eviction saga has been my initiation into the enigmatic world of the rental market. But guess what? Just like in training, it's not all sunshine, rainbows and puppies (although who wouldn't mind a puppy to live with?). I must confess, tears were shed, anger was felt, and I definitely had a moment of deflation. But then I channelled my inner trainer and thought, "What can I learn from this?" It's all about that mental switch-flipping action.

Gratitude is the secret weapon. Even in the face of an eviction, there's always something to be thankful for. It might take a little venting, a whinge, a good cry, or perhaps an eventful training session to get there, but trust me, gratitude triumphs.

So I challenge you to find your cloud of silver linings — what are the lessons, the triumphs, the good you can take from the bad moments? I'm thankful this location change gave me the opportunity to challenge my fear of the ocean, taught me how to care for some veggies, gave me a sunny outdoor flow deck, awesome Sunday walking adventures with the hubby, and put a smile on my face each morning heading out to watch a sunrise.

So here's to life's curveballs and the resilience we gain from every "First Attempt In Learning". And who knows — maybe the next rental will have an even bigger and better flow space, with puppies allowed!

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
