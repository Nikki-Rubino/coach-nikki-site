Hey,

Well, the holiday season is officially over for another year. More than likely you're in the transition phase of getting back into your usual routine — back to work hours, kids at school, weekly commitments starting up again. That's life, right!

So many times, if we feel we need more time, we'll just take it from the sleeping hours. Think about it: how many times have you stayed up late to get something done, or set a really early alarm to do it before you're disturbed? Sleep literally is the most effective thing we can do to reset our brain and body health each day.

To identify a sleep deficiency, ask yourself:

- If you didn't set an alarm clock, would you wake up on time?
- Do you find yourself re-reading things?
- Can you function optimally before noon without caffeine?
- Can you focus on the task at hand?

A handy way to think about it all:

- **Wake state** is where your brain is experiencing and constantly learning the world around you.
- **NREM (non-rapid eye movement) sleep** is reflection, where the brain stores and strengthens the day's new facts and skills.
- **REM (rapid eye movement) sleep** is the brain's integration system, interconnecting new experiences with each other and with past experiences — helping you understand how the world works, including innovative insights and problem-solving. In a nutshell, our REM sleep fuels our creativity.

Take a moment to reflect on your sleep habits. Are you even aware of how many hours of sleep your body needs to feel rejuvenated and able to function the next day? Use this week to take note of how much quality sleep you're getting, and whether it's actually enough.
