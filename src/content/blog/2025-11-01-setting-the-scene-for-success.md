Hey,

Have you ever needed to *clean your desk* before starting work, *make a coffee* before answering emails, or *put on a podcast* before heading out for a walk? Sometimes it's not the task itself that's hard — it's the *environment* we need to get right first.

For me, that lesson hit home this week during a **VO₂ max test**. If you're not familiar, it's a treadmill test that measures your aerobic fitness — basically how well your body uses oxygen at maximum effort. It starts slow, then every 3 minutes the speed or incline increases until you literally can't go anymore.

Here's the thing: I'm **not a runner**. In fact, I hate running — even more so on a treadmill, staring at a blank wall, with a mask strapped to my face making it harder to breathe. No music, no scenery, just me, silence, and a slow-burning sense of torture. Some people thrive in that environment and switch into beast mode until they drop. Me? I got *bored*. Could I have pushed harder? Probably. But without the things that motivate me — music, space to move freely, a sense of fun — I hit my mental ceiling long before my physical one.

That's the interesting thing about **motivation vs. discipline**. Motivation thrives when the environment feels inspiring or engaging. Discipline steps in when it doesn't. And sometimes we just need to *set the scene* so discipline doesn't have to do all the heavy lifting.

And it's not just the space — **people are part of our environment too**. Some lift us up, bring the energy, and make hard work feel lighter. When they're not around, you can *feel* it.

So here's to creating an environment — through space, music, and people — that's exciting, inspiring, and helps you work to your capacity on that day.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
