Hey,

We've been experiencing some crazy weather here in Sydney and Queensland over the last few weeks — flash floods, devastating communities, and just a sense of gloom. Back to indoor activities, more screen time, and not having the ability to venture out for a simple walk. I've seen a shift in upper-body stiffness, not only in my clients but also myself.

Over the next four weeks I'll be sharing simple and effective ways to use your foam roller to massage some common tight areas of the upper body.

First off: put simply, our pec has two components, the pec major and minor. Both attach at different spots but are connected to your ribcage and upper arm. When these get tight and shortened, it can be the start of a rounding posture. As an ex-TV editor, I can relate to being seated at a computer for hours at a time — paired with seated travel time and a love for boxing, my pecs and shoulders only knew a rounded forward position.

Although the pec release is only one piece of this puzzle, I share with you what may help after a day in a rounded position. Consistency is key — it takes time and repetition to transform habits that aren't of service to you, or to build new ones that are.

Give a pec release a go this week and let me know how you go.

Until next week,
Nikki
