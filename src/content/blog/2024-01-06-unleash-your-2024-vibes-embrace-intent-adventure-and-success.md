Hey,

Happy New Year!

We're already a week into 2024, and I hope it has brought you moments of joy, relaxation and inspiration. Whether you're back at work, still enjoying holidays, packing up your house, or finding time for activities that make your heart dance, let's collectively embrace the opportunities this new chapter brings.

In the midst of the "New Year, New Me" buzz, it's essential to recognise that there's nothing wrong with the person you were in the past. Instead, let's focus on nurturing habits that serve us better and taking positive actions towards your personal growth. If you have a specific goal in mind, that's fantastic — having a clear focus is commendable! (Check out the article I wrote last year on breaking down goals: "A goal without a plan is simply a wish.")

This year, let's explore a different perspective. Maybe you don't have a concrete goal in mind, or are uncertain about it. Consider defining an "intent" or "purpose" for 2024 — a word or phrase that encapsulates what you want to bring into your life. Whether it's "the year of health", "boundaries", "strength", "adventures", "connections", "success", "joy" or "education", choose something that resonates.

Your intent could be centred around how you want to feel, or the atmosphere you wish to create for others around you. Last year, my personal example was a solo month-long trip driven by the words "strength", "adventure" and "connection". The trip was to push me out of many comfort zones, further my education with my Animal Flow practice, and meet fellow global AF instructors and friends in person. I turned these words into a daily reminder by creating a laptop backdrop, serving as motivation before and after the journey.

This year, 2024, my word is "Action". Action equals do the things. Excited or scared — both are feelings with butterflies; you decide which one it is.

As we embark on this new year, let's celebrate the potential for growth, learning and joy. Whether you're pursuing a specific goal or setting an overarching intent, let 2024 be a year filled with positive vibes, self-discovery and exciting adventures. Wishing you an amazing year ahead, whatever that means for you. Feel free to share what your goals or intentions are.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
