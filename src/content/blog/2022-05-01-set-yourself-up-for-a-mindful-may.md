Hey,

This month we'll be discussing 10 things that can help flip you into a more positive vibe. Don't get me wrong — if you've met me, I can be a bundle of positive vibes (sometimes maybe a little much for an introvert), but I too am human and have my on and off days. I'm nearing 39 years of life, and within it have dealt with traumas, can get triggered, and can simply want to punch something while blasting Metallica. One thing that's helped me deal with the harder times of the year is knowing what can shift me into a better mood with not much effort required.

I'll share two tips a week. I'd love to know if anything resonates, or if you have something else to add that you've found useful for your own mood shift.

**Good vibes tip #1 — create a good morning playlist!** On whatever platform you use, choose a handful of songs that get you moving and grooving with a smile on your face. Anything you want, any vibe you want to create for that day. My hubby and I adopted this through lockdown, where every day felt like the same day — each morning we'd play a track and dance around the house.

**Good vibes tip #2 — practise gratitude.** The end of the day is a time to reflect and potentially shift the mood before you sleep. Write, say aloud, type, or simply acknowledge three things you're grateful for or that put a smile on your face that day. These can be as elaborate or as simple as you like. For example, three things I'm grateful for today: a hot morning coffee, the 15-minute walk in the sun, and a laughing chat with my bestie.

Go forth and start to find some simple, effective ways to shift your mood. Share with me anything you come across, or any morning playlist tracks — I'm always adding to mine.

Until next week,
Nikki
