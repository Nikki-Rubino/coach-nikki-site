Hey,

Do you ever feel that mix of excitement and nerves when you're about to try something new? I'm feeling exactly that right now as I get ready to kick off the first official day of an incredible Animal Flow mentorship in Bali.

It's not just about the learning — it's about pushing myself out of my comfort zone. There's about to be a lot of endurance brain and body training, and I'll need to refrain from eye-rolling, being my bratty little self, and remembering I can't pause the coach for a break like I can from the comfort of my at-home flow space. Embracing travel is always a cause of anxiety for me, but I'm excited to be experiencing it alongside a brand-new fellow Animal Flow student too!

At 41, I've realised it's never too late to start something new, ask questions, or create connections with those who are just beginning their own journeys. In fact, the more we step into those moments of uncertainty, the more we grow. And trust me, the reward is always worth the butterflies in your stomach!

Travelling (especially when it's a little nerve-wracking) is one of the best ways to shake things up. It forces you to open up, get curious, and engage with the world in a whole new way. Whether it's meeting new people, exploring new places, or just learning to navigate the unknown, every step outside your comfort zone is a chance to grow.

And that's what I love about being part of a learning community. It's not just about mastering the moves — it's about leading by example, showing that no matter your age or experience, you can keep moving forward, asking questions, and trying new things. It's never too late to begin. Now, eight years into this practice, I remember vividly my very first mentorship in 2017 in Byron — a fresh-out-of-advertising newbie, an official full-time coach of all of six weeks, spending five days with leaders in the industry who truly wanted to make the fitness space more inclusive, more educational, and to ditch the fads, the shreds and the booty-transformation crap that was infiltrating this industry. Health really is our only wealth, and that comes from so many other facets than what we deem we should "look" like.

So, if you've been thinking about starting something new (even if it feels a bit scary), take this as your nudge to just go for it! Whether it's trying a new class, learning a new skill, or embracing a new challenge — you've got this.

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
