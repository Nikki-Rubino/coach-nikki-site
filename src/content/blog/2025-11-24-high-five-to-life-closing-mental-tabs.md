Hey,

**Let's talk mental tabs.** It's that time of year when brain fog is real, the to-do list is stacked, and the end-of-year push is *pushing*. Some of you are about to clock off, some are working right through, and some are retired and expertly avoiding the crowds (teach me your ways).

The mental load is high and the adulting biz-brain is being stretched to capacity. There are only so many hours in the day, and we've all got to choose where the focus goes.

Now, onto something fun. You've heard me talk about the power of music before — but DJ Tiësto dropped a new track this week and it sent me straight down a rabbit hole of old-skool tunes. And honestly? For the past three days I've been closing mental tabs left, right, and centre — getting through tasks I'd procrastinated on for *way* too long. It's like progressive trance turns off the "I can't do this" part of my brain. Just dance about and get it done (ask the hubby, who witnessed the dance-party food prep on the weekend).

Big girl pants: on. 2026 energy: activated.

A special mention this week: **Jimmy levelled up to 90 in this game of life** — what an achievement. As part of the dazzle crew, we celebrated him unlocking a whole new level. Thank you for being such an inspiration, Jimmy. (And not to be overshadowed — Josie levelled up to 89 on the very same day!)

P.S. I saw Christmas trees up in the city, so it's not too early at all for a *Home Alone* movie marathon weekend while you put yours up too.

Stay Sassy and big love to you,
Coach Nikki
