Hey,

I thought it was time I came clean about something: just like you, I need accountability. Yep, even coaches need coaches. You might think that as someone who's been in the fitness world for years, I'm the picture of discipline, right? Wrong! If there's a choice between buckling down on what I should do and something that just sounds more fun… let's say the fun option tends to win!

Here's the thing: I know exactly what to do. I can write a program, or find the best ones out there by coaches I respect and admire. But do I stick to it? Nope. Without a solid accountability buddy to check in on me, I'll find myself wandering off course. Whether it's my Animal Flow moves or some new skill I want to master, I crave the connection and direction of a coach just like anyone else.

It's not because I don't know my stuff — it's because I'm human! We all need someone to help us stay focused, keep our motivation steady, and give us that nudge. When it comes to getting things done, I thrive on having that human element — the conversation, feedback, and encouragement that only comes with real connection.

This week I joined in on a small group session run by a fellow coach, Jake (aka Choc). We have similar values when it comes to training, and I knew I needed a little push to get back into my corrective exercises. Let's just say he witnessed the "toddler brat" side of me. Yes, I whinge, give back-chat and various eye rolls when doing something hard, even though I know it's for my own good. (Hence why I never take offence in your sessions when it happens to me. Also, turns out the myth IS true — all trainers can't count!)

So if you've been thinking, "I should be able to do this on my own," remember that even the pros need accountability. Leaning on others isn't a weakness; it's a strategy. I've had various coaches throughout the years, for all sorts of things I needed and wanted to learn — movement, finances, and business. A coach is there to guide you to where you want to go, to teach you the tools and systems, and to keep you accountable.

Where could a coach help you in the endeavours you want to pursue? If it's to get strong, flexible and more in control of your body for everyday life — let's chat! (FYI, I've been dubbed "the nurturing coach". If you're looking for the drill-sergeant type, I'm probably not your fit, but I can point you in the right direction.)

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
