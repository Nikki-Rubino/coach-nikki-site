How did the pec roll-out feel last week? I'd love to hear your experience.

This week we're focusing on the lat, aka the latissimus dorsi. If you struggle to lift your arm overhead without your ribcage pushing forward and dumping a heap of pressure into your lower back, it could be your lats restricting you.

Try this simple lat release with your foam roller. Aim for one to two minutes per side, three times. It's great for the next time you're in front of a Netflix episode or chilling to a podcast.
