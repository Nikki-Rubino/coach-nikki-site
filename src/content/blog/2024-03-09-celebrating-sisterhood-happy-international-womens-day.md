Hey,

Friday March 8th was International Women's Day — a day to honour the remarkable achievements and contributions of women around the world. As we celebrate, I want to take a moment to express my gratitude to all the incredible women who make up our global community.

To every woman reading this, thank you for being the amazing person you are. Your strength, resilience and compassion inspire those around you and contribute to building a brighter future for all. Don't believe me? Reach out to one of them!

I put out a call last week for some tuck balance coaching within the Animal Flow community, and to my surprise someone I've admired for a very long time, from New Zealand, got back to me totally happy to help. Upon meeting officially for the first time over Zoom, we shared stories of how we'd both been inspired by the other and fan-girled from afar, watching each other's flow styles. You never know who is watching and who you're inspiring, so never give up on putting yourself out there.

International Women's Day is not just about celebrating individual accomplishments; it's also about recognising the power of sisterhood. Just like in *Sisterhood of the Traveling Pants* and *Mean Girls*, we're reminded of the importance of women supporting each other through thick and thin. In *Sisterhood of the Traveling Pants*, a simple pair of pants becomes a symbol of friendship and solidarity among a group of diverse young women who come together to support and uplift each other. Similarly, in *Mean Girls*, we witness the negative consequences of tearing each other down, and the transformative power of women standing together.

As women, we have the incredible ability to create a support network that builds each other up, celebrates our successes, and helps us navigate the obstacles we face. Whether it's through mentorship, friendship, or simply lending a listening ear, our support for one another is invaluable.

So, on this International Women's Day weekend, let's celebrate the bonds of sisterhood that unite us. Let's commit to being there for each other, lifting each other up, and paving the way for a future where all women can thrive.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
