Hey,

Happy summer, and can you believe it's been a week already? I hope this email finds you in good spirits, and possibly with less hair dye on your hands than I currently have in my hair. It's officially summer, and guess what? Last night was our gym's 90s Christmas bash — ah, the nostalgia!

Speaking of getting things done, I can totally relate to the pre-summer chaos. It's like a marathon, but with sunscreen and BBQs. Last week we talked about the mental load of this time of year, and today I'm here to shout from the virtual rooftops: any movement is better than no movement. We're all in this together, tackling our summer to-do lists like champs.

Time is of the essence. Between cooking, buying, preparing and socialising, it's a whirlwind out there. But it's also an opportunity to create lasting memories with friends, family and loved ones. And let's not forget about making ourselves a priority in the midst of the summer hustle.

So picture yourself as a teapot (a fabulous one, of course). You can only pour into others if you're full yourself. Now, don't underestimate the power of:

- A 15-minute walk — bonus points if it involves a sunrise, sunset or some lovely nature vibes.
- 10 minutes of breathwork or meditation — channel your inner zen master for a quick mental reset.
- 10 minutes walking uphill, on a beach, or any uneven surface — embrace the challenge and stimulate those muscles.
- Playtime with kids, mates or colleagues — laughter is the best medicine, they say!
- Lifting something heavy — your kids, a suitcase, or even some pot plants. Get that muscle tissue moving.
- A smile — amidst the chaos, a simple smile can work wonders, not just for you but for others around you.

I know it feels like a circus out there, especially in the chaotic world of holiday shopping. But trust me, a smile can be a game-changer. It has the power to lift your own mood and brighten someone else's day.

Life is a whirlwind right now, but let's make a pact to make ourselves a priority. Together, we'll conquer the rest of 2023 with positive vibes and a sense of accomplishment. So let's hustle, smile, and make this summer one for the books! Wishing you a fantastic weekend filled with joy, laughter, and maybe a dance move or two from the 90s.

Cheers to us, and to rocking the finish line of 2023!

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
