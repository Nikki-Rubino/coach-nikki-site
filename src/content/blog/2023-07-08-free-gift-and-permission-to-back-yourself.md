Hey,

This week is all about taking time to listen to your instincts and trust yourself.

Chatting with a fellow coach this week who was struggling to plan her first upcoming event was actually so eye-opening. I know I'm guilty of overthinking and trying to have the whole plan mapped out before I even begin, or seeing the picture too large and getting overwhelmed very easily. Can you relate?

Yet here I was giving some sound concepts of how to tackle her roadblocks of indecision, when I had to pause, giggle — and three other people in the room knew exactly why. They know I too need to get out of my own way and tell these concepts to myself. They simply said: record yourself right now and play this back to you.

Think about it. How are you standing in your own way? Is it fear? Do you need help from someone in that field? Are you trying to make it perfect? Even writing this email this week, I was plagued with indecision and self-doubt — the inner voice saying "do these even help anyone?", "no one will read it anyway", and "just go train with Pauly".

Step one: tell the voice, "Oh, that's a nice observation, thanks — but I'll write this anyway." Then, yes, go train.

You can do more than you give yourself credit for. Back yourself. Break down a step one. What can you do right now? What do you need to learn, and how? Then do step one. Once the ball is rolling, you fill in the gaps, I promise.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
