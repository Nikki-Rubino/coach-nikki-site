Hey,

Well hello, sunshine — literally. Did anyone else feel like they finally *defrosted* this week? Cabin fever was real, and I was *definitely* in the thick of it.

Between the cold, dreary days, trying to wrangle an outdoor event on soggy grass, and spending the weekend with a personality that pushed *all* the wrong buttons… let's just say my battery was low, my anxiety was high, and my patience left on the same flight as Tima (who's off spending time with her fam, leaving me solo at the studio).

And apparently my face gave me away. About a dozen people commented: "You look flat." "Oooh, I haven't met this side of you before — usually it's sunshine and rainbows!" Yep, turns out I'm human too. Years in hospitality and advertising gave me a solid "service face," but when the battery is empty, even that starts to crack.

So, what did I do? I chased the recharge moments:

- Soaked up the sunshine when climbing plans got swapped for a city adventure.
- Got in some movement I needed to memorise (because no space indoors).
- Pupnapped my folks' dog for couch cuddles while hubby was away.

And you know what? It worked. Because here's the science-y bit: movement *literally* changes your mood. It boosts dopamine — your feel-good neurotransmitter — which helps shake off stress, recharge your brain, and remind you that you're more than just a grumpy lizard wishing for a rock to sunbathe on.

So whenever life feels a little… much, reach for your movement snacks. Go get your movement, go get your dopamine, and go get your mood boost.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
