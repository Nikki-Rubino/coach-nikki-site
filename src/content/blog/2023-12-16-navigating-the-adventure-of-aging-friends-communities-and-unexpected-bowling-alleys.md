Hey,

Getting older can be a bit like tackling an obstacle course without the agility of your youth. Simple plans suddenly involve more logistics than a military operation. However, fear not! Friends are the unsung heroes who navigate these challenges with you, turning mundane tasks into shared adventures.

On the flip side, communities are like the supportive background music to your life. Whether it's your training buddies, trivia nights or book clubs, these gatherings provide a comforting sense of belonging, making the ageing process more of a lively fiesta and less of a solo expedition. In a world where technology races ahead, genuine connections remain timeless.

Take our recent Christmas party, for example. A heatwave, a bowling alley overrun by sugar-hyped kids, and a voiceless host created unexpected hiccups. Yet the diverse group, ranging from mid-20s to late 80s, all connected through training, embraced the situation. They interacted, encouraged each other, and were there for some fun — a true testament to finding your tribe.

So embrace the quirks, value the laughter, and savour the friendships and communities that add flavour to the ageing journey. Cheers to growing older with good company, a healthy dose of humour and a playful heart!

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
