Hey,

"Change is the only constant in life. Embracing the unknown can lead to growth beyond what you ever imagined."

Well, the butterflies in my belly were on point this week! Tuesday rolled around, and stepping back into sessions felt like being the new kid in the first year of school. I may still be with the same amazing people, but the environment is different. Who are my teachers? Where will I sit in this classroom? Do I have all the necessary equipment with me? I hadn't felt this mild sense of nervous excitement in a long time… and to be honest, it felt good. It was exciting, challenging, and a clear reminder that this is just the start of a brand-new chapter!

It got me thinking about how similar this feeling is to starting something new in training, or releasing an old habit that's been comforting but might not be serving us well anymore. Is that habit or environment serving you — or limiting you?

Sometimes, we need a little shake-up to wake up. Don't worry, I'm not suggesting you drastically overhaul your life (unless you want to, in which case, grab life and go for it!). But even small shifts can spark big changes.

Looking back on my week, it's been incredible. Everyone's been so supportive, and while there were a few teething issues here and there, they've been pretty minor in the grand scheme of things. Like the time I went to heat up lunch. Microwave? Check. Food? Check. Cutlery? Oh, wait — nope. Definitely not finger-food friendly! Or when I needed to hotspot my phone for a virtual session and realised… no charger. Little things, but they made me chuckle.

Overall, the sessions have been buzzing, the vibes are high, and the feedback so far has been amazing. We've created a welcoming, inviting space, and apparently the music playlists are a hit too!

So, as 2025 kicks off, here's my takeaway: change is OK. Shedding something that isn't serving you? That's OK. Stepping out of your comfort zone to see what you're capable of? That's OK too.

Start small. Whether it's trying a new training location, adding a little extra load to your deadlift, or simply walking a different route to soak in some new scenery, you never know what's around the corner. Each small step can lead to something bigger — your next move towards owning your health and wellbeing.

Here's to embracing what's next!

Take care, stay Sassy, and let's ring in the new year slightly out of our comfort zone 💛
Nikki
