Hey,

I'm still buzzing from last weekend's **Coach Nikki Christmas Party 2025** — what an afternoon.

As a collective, I call this tribe the **Dazzle Crew**, because a group of zebras is called a dazzle. Each zebra has its own unique stripes, just like every human who walks through my doors. Together, they form social herds built on trust, safety, and connection — and that's exactly what this crew embodies.

Nine legends weren't pictured, but absolutely not forgotten. Not everyone knew each other, but watching everyone come together — laughing, playing, bantering, and genuinely vibing under one roof — filled this lil heart right up.

This year has been a wild one: my first in a co-shared space, no longer under anyone else's umbrella. It's been scary, unknown, and at times completely overwhelming. But looking at these faces — from the OGs of nine years to the fresh new additions — I'm reminded why I do what I do. You lot make it all worthwhile.

Because at the end of the day, **strength and community are two of the biggest ingredients for longevity** — they keep our bodies capable, our minds resilient, and our hearts full.

So here's something to take into your week: **reach out to a training buddy or a feel-good friend** — go for a walk, share a coffee, or simply check in. Movement is powerful, but connection makes it last.

Here's to community, courage, and continuing to dazzle into 2026.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
