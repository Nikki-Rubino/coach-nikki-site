Hi Friend,

**Welcome to the new year.** A fresh start, a clean slate.

Now let's be honest — how many times have you set goals, intentions, motivations (whatever you choose to call them) on New Year's Day, or at least through January? Something along the lines of *this year I will… this year I want to… this is the year to…* Lose weight, quit my job, eat healthily, exercise more, start pilates, do a course, travel, see my family more, save money, get "toned", start my hobby, join a club.

Now, how many times have you actually followed through? If you have — awesome! If you haven't, what stopped you? For a lot of people, and even myself, there can be a few reasons. The top two:

1. **Not realising the deeper meaning behind the want or goal.**
2. **Not having a clear plan of action or accountability.**

Breaking it into bite-sized chunks gives you one small task to implement, and a useful way of setting goals and building the habits to get there — no matter what the goal is. (Two resources I lean on: the book *Atomic Habits* by James Clear, and the *Daily Business Coach* podcast by Fiona Kallacky.)

Sometimes it's as simple as asking yourself the right questions. Where do you want to go — or more importantly, **what person do you want to become?** Ponder this sentence:

## I want to get from "X" to "Y" by "WHEN" so that "WHY"

- **X** = your current situation
- **Y** = where you want to go
- **WHEN** = when you want to arrive at Y
- **WHY** = what this enables you to do, and how it makes you feel

I'll use a personal example — we're all friends here, right? I want to get **from needing a wall to handstand** to **holding a 10-second free-standing handstand** by **May 5th** so that **I can feel more confident holding a tuck handstand for the Level 3 Animal Flow workshop I want to attend in America.**

You can see where I'm currently at, where I want to be, a realistic deadline, and a greater emotional purpose. That WHY will be the driving force.

Now you try. What do you truly want for yourself this year, and **why**? Be as specific as you can. Feel free to share, or simply keep it to yourself.
