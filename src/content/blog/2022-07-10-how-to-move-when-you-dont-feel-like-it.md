Hey,

How are you trucking through the wet, cold days that have been upon us?

I'll admit, with selling my apartment and getting documents sorted to start rental applications with a deadline looming, the body is feeling run down. The motivation to move and lift heavy is not high right now. This can leave me feeling like a fraud at times — being at Jungle Brothers each day, I'm surrounded by young, motivated coaches getting in their sets and reps and making gains. It's easy to go down a rabbit hole of negative thoughts.

What I'm realising is that, at this time, I simply have different priorities taking my attention. I'm slowing things down because mentally I'm drained, and that's totally okay. It's a win if I can get out for a walk, or do 20 to 30 minutes of Animal Flow drills and practice.

Life can throw curveballs; we're never going to be in balance 100% of the time. The main thing is to assess whether this is a short-term scenario. I realise mine is, so it's okay. But if there are external factors affecting your sleep, nutrition and mental health, and there's no end in sight, then it may be time to start assessing what you can change. Any movement is better than no movement. Our bodies need to move to feel nourished.

As coaches, we're not superheroes. We're simply human, and sometimes our motivation is low too.

I'm curious — what inspires you to move?

Until next week,
Nikki
