This week I want to share some of her findings about the power of posture. So let's go.

**The power of body language**

Amy Cuddy's groundbreaking research reveals that our body language not only influences how others perceive us but also affects how we feel about ourselves. In her book *Presence*, Cuddy emphasises the importance of "power posing" — adopting expansive, open postures to boost our confidence and presence.

**Meet the starfish: your new confidence pose**

One of the most popular power poses Cuddy highlights is the "starfish up". Imagine a starfish with its limbs spread wide, taking up space confidently. When we mimic this stance, our bodies send signals to our brains, making us feel more powerful and assertive.

**Why "starfish up" works**

- **Hormonal shifts:** adopting a power pose for just two minutes can increase testosterone (the dominance hormone) and decrease cortisol (the stress hormone). This shift helps you feel more confident and less anxious.
- **Mind-body connection:** our bodies and minds are deeply interconnected. When we hold a confident posture, our minds start to believe it, leading to genuine feelings of empowerment.
- **Social perception:** people perceive us as more confident and capable when we occupy more space with our bodies. This can positively impact our interactions and opportunities.

**The contrast: slouched posture**

When we hunch over, make ourselves small, and cross our arms or legs, our bodies send signals to our brains that we are less confident and more vulnerable. This can lead to increased feelings of anxiety and a decrease in perceived power, both in ourselves and in those around us.

(Side note: check out *Inside Out 2* — yes, I am a grown adult who went to the movies to see a kids' flick — and look at the posture of embarrassment and sadness compared to anger and joy.)

**How to "starfish up"**

- **Stand tall:** plant your feet firmly on the ground, shoulder-width apart.
- **Spread out:** extend your arms wide, reaching up as if you're celebrating a big victory. Or simply hands on hips, elbows, chest and ribcage as W I D E as you can.
- **Breathe deep:** take a few deep breaths, filling your lungs and expanding your chest.
- **Hold:** maintain this pose for two minutes, embracing the power and confidence it brings.

**When to use it**

- **Morning routine:** imagine jumping straight out of bed into a starfish, ready to tackle the day ahead.
- **Before important meetings or starting something new:** step into a private space and starfish up to boost your confidence.
- **During stressful situations:** use this pose to calm your nerves and regain control.

Why not give it a try? Take a moment now to stand up, spread out, and feel the power of the starfish. You might be surprised at how such a simple change can make a big difference.

You are enough and you matter. Keep standing tall and shining bright!
