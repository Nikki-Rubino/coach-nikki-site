Hey,

How is that upper body feeling? You've learned a basic way of releasing through your chest, back and shoulders, and you may be able to move a little better as a result.

This week is about using all of the range you've created over the past three weeks, and getting a little deeper. Remember, there are many ways to gain mobility, and using a foam roller is simply one tool.

Welcome to the Sliding W. As a preface: if this feels super tight or causes any pinching, simply attempt to lie in the W position without a slide, or lay your arms out into a T position instead, until you're feeling ready. The foam roller gives you tactile feedback — as you drive your hands up, feel the foam roller under your lower back, notice if your ribcage starts to flare, and pull your back along for the ride.

Aim for three sets of eight to 10 slides, followed by a 60-second hold in the W position. I'd love to know if you felt a difference after doing any of the Mobility March drills.
