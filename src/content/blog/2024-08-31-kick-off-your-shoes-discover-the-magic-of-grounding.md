Hey,

Well, spring feels are definitely upon us in Sydney this week. Who else is excited to finally free their lil feet from shoes and socks, to soak up the warm surface, different textures, and feel good to be grounded again? Or is it just me?

Ever wondered why walking barefoot on the grass feels so good? It's not just in your head — there's some real magic going on! Here's why grounding (or earthing) can be a game-changer:

**Instant mood booster.** Stress got you feeling frazzled? Grounding is like hitting the reset button. By connecting with nature, you can lower stress hormones, lift your spirits, and find your inner calm. It's certainly part of my morning routine — I feel horrible if I miss out on a rocky sunrise adventure near some water.

**Zap away inflammation.** When you kick off your shoes and plant those feet on the earth, you're soaking up natural electrons that help neutralise pesky free radicals. This can calm down inflammation and keep you feeling tip-top.

**Sweet dreams.** Struggling to catch those Zzz's? When you connect with the Earth, it can help reset your internal clock (fancy term: circadian rhythm), making it easier to drift off and stay there.

So next time you're near a patch of grass or a sandy beach, kick off those shoes and get grounded — you might just find a new favourite way to unwind. Let me know what texture you prefer!

I do find it alarming how many people don't feel safe, confident, or comfortable being barefoot. So this week, I shared a snippet of my latest (and first ever) webinar with four tools to help you build confidence and feel safe on your own two feet.
