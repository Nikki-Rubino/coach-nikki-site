Hey,

As the year winds down, it's time to hit pause and reflect — not just on the big wins but on the quiet victories too. Sure, some people might be shouting about their marathons, promotions, or record-breaking lifts. But let's not forget: showing up, day in and day out, is a win worth celebrating too. 💪

**What did you really achieve?** Maybe you started a new routine. Maybe you didn't quit that routine when it got tough. Maybe you gave yourself the grace to rest when you needed it. If you managed to lace up your shoes, get out your foam roller, or even just remind yourself to breathe deeply when the day got chaotic, you accomplished more than you think.

**The science of showing up.** Research tells us consistency — not perfection — is what builds lasting habits. Every small choice adds up. Every "meh" workout, every "I'll just do 10 minutes," and every "okay, let's try again tomorrow" moment lays the foundation for long-term progress. So, if you're tempted to brush off your efforts this year, don't. Every step counts.

**My favourite wins this year.** As I look back on 2024, I'm grateful for: hosting community-building events like Glow Flow in Sydney and QLD; starting swimming again (even if I get distracted mid-lap); presenting on stage at the Fuel Women in Fitness Business Summit; the opportunity to attend AF Mentorship in Bali; morning routines of sunrise dance sessions on a rock by the sea (a boundary I've set to recharge so I can give my best for the rest of the day); and taking a leap into new opportunities, like opening a new co-shared studio in January. Your support — whether you joined a session, liked a post, or simply read these emails — has meant the world to me. Thank you.

**Looking ahead to 2025.** Here's the thing: you don't need to reinvent yourself on January 1. The "new year, new you" mantra? Overrated. Instead, aim for "new year, same amazing you, but maybe with a little extra spice." 🌶️

**This week's challenge.** Take five minutes to list your wins for 2024. Big, small, or somewhere in between — they all count.

Here's to showing up, staying strong, and moving forward. P.S. Remember, your effort is enough. You are enough.

Take care, stay Sassy, and let's ring in the new year with gratitude 💛
Nikki
