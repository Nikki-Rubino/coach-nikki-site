Hey,

Have you ever felt like your mind is a bit of a bully? You're trying to do one thing, but your brain is somewhere else entirely, throwing distractions your way. Maybe you're walking and trip on a curb, or suddenly lose balance during an exercise. It's these moments when tuning into our body is essential — and, honestly, it's the only thing we can truly control.

For me, Animal Flow is my go-to practice for this kind of body awareness. But there are plenty of ways to find that connection, even when you're just tuning into music with your headphones on. It's all about learning to cut through the external noise and listen to what your body is trying to tell you.

A personal example? Swimming. Recently, I've been challenging myself to hit 20 laps. Now, let me tell you, silence is not my strong suit! My mind quickly wanders to my to-do list, scary memories of my childhood swim coach, or even the "Jaws" theme (don't ask!). When I lose focus, the laps feel endless, my strokes get sloppy, and I get exhausted fast.

But when I catch myself and bring the focus back to my body, things change. I'll focus on my arm strokes in the shallow end, my leg kicks from the deep, and I even use markers to see how far I can go each time I push off. It's a simple but powerful mental shift, and it makes all the difference. It even helps me sleep better, especially when I focus on opening up my tight right side and activating my left hip — a little fix for my "mangled gremlin" sleep posture!

It's a good reminder that whatever movement we're doing, be it in training or just day-to-day, we need to tune into our bodies. One of my clients nailed it when he said, "If I don't think of all the points, the wheels fall off, and the wagon (that's me) might fall over!"

What's an activity that helps you bring awareness to your body? How do you tune in and take control?

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
