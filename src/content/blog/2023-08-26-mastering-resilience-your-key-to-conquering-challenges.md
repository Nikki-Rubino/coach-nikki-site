Hey,

Resilience — the ability to bounce back, adapt, and recover from setbacks, challenges and difficult situations.

I'm so proud of tribe members and PTs this week. We had curveballs thrown at us, with the usual space being taken over for a TV shoot, three coaches out of action, and a few changes in schedules. It could have been an easy way out to drop everything and take time off. In the Animal Flow discipline, one of our four pillars is resilience, and it's something that really shone through this week in how smoothly everyone adapted, kept up the routine, and embraced a new environment.

Here's why resilience is such a great quality in both training and life:

**Dealing with challenges.** Life is full of challenges, setbacks and failures. Resilience equips you with the mental and emotional strength to face these head-on. In training, it's common to encounter obstacles that hinder progress. Resilience helps you push through plateaus, injuries and other setbacks, enabling you to continue towards your goals.

**Mental wellbeing.** Resilience plays a significant role in maintaining good mental health. It helps manage stress and anxiety by generating a positive mindset. Resilient individuals are better equipped to handle the ups and downs of life without succumbing to prolonged negative emotions.

**Adaptability.** The world is constantly changing, and being adaptable is essential for success. Resilience allows you to quickly adjust to new circumstances, learn from experiences, and embrace change.

And remember — you don't have to do things alone. Build a supportive network. Surround yourself with positive, supportive people who encourage your growth and wellbeing. Having friends, family, mentors or colleagues to lean on during tough times can provide valuable emotional support.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
