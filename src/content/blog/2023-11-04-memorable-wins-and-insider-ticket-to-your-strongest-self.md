Hey,

So November is upon us. Can you believe it?

This week has been a load of positives to share. Last week I wrote about how small consistencies create long-lasting wins, and I'm excited for the last quarter of 2023. As we head into the back end of the year, I realise schedules get full — there's more on the to-do list with families, friends, kids and work. It's the habits you've cultivated throughout the year that are going to serve you now.

This week I'm happy to share wins from clients themselves. A feedback form went out, and one question was: "Can you share any memorable or standout moments you're proud of since starting your sessions?"

"I can see improvement each week, especially in my coordination and ability to complete exercises. I was very pleased to discover all the exercises linked to my problems and have a clear idea of what to work on. Thankfully we are taking small steps towards large gains!" — Kerry

"My daughter saying I now have some muscles! Being able to confidently walk up and down stairs again. Walking the High Line in New York." — Anne

"Getting out of soggy Wentworth Park by getting over a brick wall. Being able to look after my own luggage in the overhead cabin bins when flying. Opening jars! Able to lift much heavier objects than before. Climbing steep steps in Glebe."

All of these legends are within the 60s and 70s levels of life category. It's never too late to start working on yourself for your strongest future self!

This is the time to keep going and know that your own health and fitness are still a priority. Haven't started yet and looking to cultivate those habits? Simply reach out.
