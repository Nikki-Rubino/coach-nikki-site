Hey,

I'm writing to you from beautiful Bali!

It's been an incredible week so far, filled with new techniques, fresh insights, and — perhaps most importantly — meaningful conversations with an amazing group of people. There's such a fantastic mix here: gym owners, coaches, physios, chiropractors, sports athletes, CrossFitters, yoga practitioners, and fitness enthusiasts from all walks of life!

What makes this experience so rich is the exchange of ideas between people who approach fitness and movement from so many different angles. I've had eye-opening discussions that have broadened my understanding of how we can all improve our bodies, and how strength and mobility benefit us in different ways. It's a reminder that no matter where you are in your fitness journey, learning from others adds layers to your own growth.

This diversity in perspective is why learning from those you admire — and even from peers in different fields — is so powerful. It speeds up our progress, gives us new tools to work with, and inspires us to look at things differently. I can't wait to bring back what I've learned and introduce you to new techniques that will benefit your own strength and mobility journey. I'm forever grateful for the calibre of coaches I've interacted with, even this early on in my career transition eight years ago.

If these conversations have taught me one thing, it's that learning never stops. No matter how experienced we are, there's always something new we can pick up to improve our bodies and our lives. When I get back, we'll dive into all of this together!

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
