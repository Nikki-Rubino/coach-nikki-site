Hey,

Are you ready to revolutionise the way we approach fitness? Let's dive into a topic that's not just about reps and sets but about collaboration, respect and understanding. We're all in this together, after all!

In the vibrant world of fitness, it's easy to get caught up in our own methods and perspectives. Think of the CrossFitters versus yogis versus powerlifters versus marathon runners versus calisthenics versus Muay Thai fighters versus flowists — there's a plethora of movement options out there. It's not a "my way or the highway" scenario, or at least it shouldn't be! When we isolate ourselves within our niche, we miss out on the rich tapestry of knowledge that others bring to the table.

As personal trainers, we're often viewed as the frontline soldiers in the battle for better health. But our role extends far beyond the confines of the gym, and the truth is we hold just one piece of the puzzle. To unlock the full potential of our clients, we must recognise the value in collaboration. A few of you reading this right now have a collab with myself and Coach Tima from JB's, or I'm working in conjunction with your physio, or encouraging you to get a massage from Crystal. I don't understand the need to "safeguard" and "claim" people as "yours". If I know there's someone better suited for the outcome you want, why not put you both in touch?

There were a few instances in the past few weeks of people across industries getting defensive when a client was seeing someone from another field. Heck, I had a website enquiry this week wanting mobile personal training — I wasn't the perfect fit due to my schedule, so I sent her on over to my previous company. We're all in this together, and should simply want the best outcome for you.

Here's a framework worth taking on board:

**Holistic perspective.** Picture a mosaic, with each piece representing a different discipline — chiropractic care, physiotherapy, personal training, massage therapy, breathwork and more. Each contributes to the overall picture of health, but none can stand alone.

**Unique bodies, unique solutions.** Just as no two puzzle pieces are the same, no two bodies are identical. What works for one person may not work for another. Rather than trying to fit everyone into the same mould, let's tailor our strategies to meet individuals where they are.

**Strength in unity.** Imagine the impact we could have if we joined forces, combining our expertise to create comprehensive wellness plans that address every aspect of someone's health journey. By working together, we amplify our effectiveness.

So how can we put this into practice? It starts with a shift in mindset. Let's set aside our egos and preconceptions, and instead approach each other with curiosity and openness. Seek out opportunities for collaboration — through workshops, joint sessions, or simply respectful dialogue.

Knowledge is power. Take control of your body, and if you're visiting a health professional and don't quite understand what you're working towards or why, maybe it's time to find a different calibre, or simply have someone to ask to break things down. You only have one true home — your body — and it's up to you to know how to take care of it.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
