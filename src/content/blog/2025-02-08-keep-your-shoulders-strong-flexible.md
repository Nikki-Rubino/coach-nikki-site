I just wanted to drop in with a fun reminder about something super important — our shoulders! If I'm honest, I've always felt self-conscious about my broad shoulders; the spaghetti-strap era was not my favourite trend. But these days I'm grateful for the shoulder strength and mobility that's been accumulating.

As a female, as we waltz through life's eras — at level 41 — I'm very thankful for the awareness around perimenopause. Why? A little drop in estrogen can mean our joints might get a bit inflamed. And since our shoulders are like the ultimate multi-taskers with all their angles and moves, keeping them strong and flexible is a must.

Think about it: our shoulders do so much, from letting us put overhead luggage up on our next adventure, to taking up the new craze of pickleball if we want to, and I know I'm always thankful for that shoulder mobility in any bathroom stop alone in a back-zipped jumpsuit.

This month, I'm gearing up to share some cool shoulder tips and tricks to keep you feeling agile and strong. From fun stretches to easy exercises, we're going to make shoulder care a blast. One movement you can start with anywhere is the seated ape reach + handcuffs.

Got any burning questions or shoulder secrets you're curious about? I'm all ears.
