Hey,

I'm here to share my top five tips on how to embrace the early mornings. I'm usually awake at 4:45am, so trust me, these techniques take some practice, but you do finally adapt.

**1. Make an appointment with yourself before you go to bed.** If you know you're training the following morning, make it a mental priority and acknowledgement before bed. That way you already know your plan of action for the morning.

**2. Get ready.** Leave out your workout clothes, drink bottle and towel so you see them as soon as you wake — a visual representation of how you're starting your day. If you head straight to work after your session, pack that bag too.

**3. Get to bed early.** I used to work night shift, so I understand there's an adjustment period. If you're used to late nights, it's understandable you want to chill and sleep in. Try this for one week: go to bed 10 minutes earlier each night. Just 10 minutes. By the end of that week, boom — you've gained yourself an extra hour of glorious sleep!

**4. Prepare.** I've said it before and I'll say it again: prepare your meals. A couple of hours on the weekend saves you a lot of time later. Boil a heap of eggs to have on hand. Cook a chosen protein, grain and veggies in bulk, so you have a go-to to chuck in a bowl with some good fats for a quick dinner, or layer in a jar for a portable lunch.

**5. Listen to your inner cheerleader.** We all have her, and we all have her opposition. I envision them as my inner cheerleader and my inner emo. My cheerleader is always positive, reminding me how awesome I feel after a movement session, and of the goals I have. My emo is on the dark side — she wants the snooze button, to stay under the doona, and finds aches and sniffles that may not necessarily be there. We start every day with choices, and these choices set our intention for the rest of the day. Remember tip one: you made an appointment with yourself. You have goals you want to achieve, so fire up and go achieve them!

Remember, the hardest part of your workout is getting out of bed. Let your inner cheerleader prevail.

Nikki
