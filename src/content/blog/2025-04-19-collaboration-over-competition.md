Hey,

The fitness industry can be… baffling.

I'm lucky. Early on, I met mentors who genuinely wanted to see me thrive. No ego, just open doors and knowledge. That path led me away from influencer-land and into the world of *real coaching* — mentoring, sharing, collaborating.

Last week reminded me why that matters so much. We ran a breath and sound healing event, and the studio was buzzing. Ten familiar faces, four new ones — but all connected. Some came through Simon, some through me. And I kept hearing, "I've heard so much about you — I wasn't going to miss this!" This is what the wellness space *should* be — people lifting each other up, not competing for likes or clients.

Take our studio. Tima's all things Muay Thai and kettlebells. I'm Animal Flow, sassy seniors, and helping people understand their own bodies. We coach differently — and proudly refer people to each other when it's the better fit. Some train with both of us, for different reasons. No drama. Just mutual respect.

So if you're on your own health journey, find a coach who *collaborates* and *educates*, not one who gatekeeps or grandstands.

*"If you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together."*

Keep moving and stay Sassy,
Nikki
