Hey,

Today I wanted to take a moment to send some love and give a massive shout out to each and every type of mumma bear out there, past and present. Thank you for being the incredible forces of nature that you are! I'm grateful to still have my mumma bear as a constant in my life, and I feel she is the epitome of all the mumma figures out there. So whichever category you fall into, here's a cheers to you.

To all the biological mums out there: you are the heartbeat of your family, the rock that holds everything together. Your love knows no bounds, and your strength is unmatched. Keep shining your light!

Big ups to the step mums: your role is unique and invaluable. You embrace your family with open arms and an open heart, weaving love into every corner of your blended family's life.

Let's not forget our fur baby mums: your pets are not just animals, they're family. Your love and care for them bring joy and companionship into your life every day.

And to the plant mums: your green thumb knows how to nurture and bring life to every leaf and petal. Your home is a sanctuary of greenery and growth.

Shout out to all the cool aunts out there: you bring laughter, fun and wisdom into your nieces' and nephews' lives. You're the go-to person for adventures, advice and unconditional love.

A special mention to our female mentors: you're guiding lights, inspirations and sources of wisdom for the next generation. Your support and encouragement empower others to reach for the stars.

To those who have experienced the unimaginable pain of losing a child: your strength in the face of such loss is awe-inspiring. Your love endures, and your memories are cherished forever.

And to those who are on the journey to becoming a mum — whether it's through pregnancy, adoption, or other means — your courage, hope and determination are truly inspiring. Keep believing; your time will come.

So, mumma bears, whatever type you may be, take a moment today to do whatever makes your heart dance. Whether it's a quiet moment of self-care, a silly dance party with your loved ones, or simply basking in the warmth of the sun, remember that you are appreciated, cherished and celebrated.

Keep roaring with love and joy, mumma bears!

(And a special shout out to my own mumma bear, Beano — selfless, caring, and always there for us and so many others.)

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
