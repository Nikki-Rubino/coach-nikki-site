Hey,

Change is a strange thing, isn't it? One moment it's exciting, the next, utterly terrifying. But here's the thing I remind myself: excitement and fear are simply both butterflies in the belly — it's all about how we choose to interpret them.

After much reflection (and a few sleepless nights), I've made a significant decision: I'm leaving the place I've called my work home for the past five years, Jungle Botany. It's been a part of my life since 2016, and leaving feels like the end of an era. But as bittersweet as it is, I know this is the right step.

Einstein once said, "Life is like riding a bicycle. To keep your balance, you must keep moving." And moving is exactly what I'm doing — not away from what I've built, but toward something new that will challenge me, help me grow, and give me the freedom to continue serving the people I love to coach.

This isn't my first leap into the unknown. In 2017, I left the security of a TV editor role in advertising to coach full-time, driven by my passion for helping others move, grow, and thrive. Back then, I had no idea how things would turn out. I started with nothing but a drive to make it work. And while it was scary, it was also one of the best decisions of my life. Now, I step forward again — not starting from scratch this time, but with lessons, skills, and milestones under my belt that prove I can do this.

Change is never easy, but it's the catalyst for growth. It pushes us to adapt, learn, and experience the new. Sure, there are moments when I wonder if I'm crazy, but I know deep down that the next chapter is waiting, full of potential and possibility.

To all of you navigating your own changes — whether it's a new job, a shift in routine, or just trying something unfamiliar — I'm here to remind you: those butterflies in your belly? They're a good sign. They mean you're on the edge of something big.

Stay tuned for what's next. I can't wait to share this new chapter with you!

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
