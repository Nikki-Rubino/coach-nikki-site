Hey,

As the year starts to wind down, I want to remind you of something very important: it's okay to take a break. It's okay to slow down. It's okay to stop hustling for five hot minutes.

The world loves to shout *"go harder, do more, don't stop!"* And sure — sometimes we're here for that energy. But if you're feeling tired, mentally full, a little heavy, or just craving space… rest is not quitting. It's recharging.

I've also made my **annual end-of-year wrap-up video**, because reflecting on how far you've come matters just as much as what's next. And let's be honest — all I want for Christmas is gains (the sustainable, feel-good kind).

Here's the thing though, and this is where the magic is: it's not what we do *between Christmas and New Year* that really counts. It's what we do *between New Year and Christmas* — the small, consistent choices, the showing up, the care for our bodies and minds over time.

So take the nap. Eat the food. Move if it feels good. Don't move if it doesn't. Come back refreshed, not fried.

I'm proud of you for everything you've shown up for this year — especially the days it felt hard. See you on the other side, recharged and ready.

Stay Sassy and big love to you,
Coach Nikki
