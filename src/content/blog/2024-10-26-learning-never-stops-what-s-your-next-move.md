Hey,

Have you ever noticed how kids are constantly learning new things? As we age, we often forget how much joy there is in being a student of life. But I'm here to remind you — we can (and should!) always be learning.

I love who I get to coach from a learning perspective, too. The majority of my PT Ninjas are more life-experienced than me, and each and every session I'll learn something new, as much as they learn movement technique from me. After some time off, I'm so excited to be getting back into sessions this week.

Whether it's through Animal Flow, strength training, or just staying curious about our bodies, continuing to learn keeps us young at heart. It's not about perfection; it's about the process. When we embrace this mindset, we become more adaptable, resilient, and ready for anything life throws our way. (Even if it's just keeping up with the Gen Alpha lingo of "skibidi rizz Ohio" 🙈.)

I'll be bringing plenty of new learning opportunities, and I'm ready to kick off some new ideas that are brewing in the not-so-distant future. So I ask you: what is something you wish to know more about from the health and fitness realm?

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
