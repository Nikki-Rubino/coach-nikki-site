Hey,

So… fun little plot twist from last weekend in Gympie. Right as we all dropped down into Loaded Beast (yes, face to the floor, bums in the air), the ground decided to shake with a 5.2 magnitude earthquake. Nothing like the earth literally moving beneath you to remind you that *movement is life*.

I was there for 16 hours of learning, sharing, and growing with my QLD flow fam and a fellow Regional Leader from the Philippines. Honestly, this community never fails to bring out the smiles, inspo, and so many movement memories.

And while I was busy being reminded how powerful movement is, I know a lot of us back home have been feeling the opposite lately. With all this relentless rain, it's been easy to feel *sluggish, achey, and borderline over it*. You are not alone — we even joked after Thursday night's classes that everyone was just "cold, grumpy, and stuck inside." Cabin fever is real, my friends.

That's where your movement snacks come in. Tiny bites of movement that cut through the slump, loosen up your joints, and remind your body it's still capable of more than Netflix marathons.

Do one a day. Do them all in a row. Do them in your slippers. Just *do them*. Because a little movement is the perfect antidote to the rainy-day blues.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
