Buckle in, this week we're chatting injuries!

Injuries are an inevitable part of any active lifestyle, and while they can feel like a major setback, they don't have to define your journey. Instead of letting an injury consume you, let's focus on what we can control and use this time to rebuild ourselves from the inside out.

You may be aware that over a month ago a routine chiro session set off a back pain I had never experienced. It was debilitating — everything was a nervey and shocking pain that shot either up my back or down my left leg. No position was comfortable: sitting, standing, walking or even sleeping. A client had to loan me a heat pack that strapped to me so I could get some sort of comfort running sessions in the gym. There is never a great time for an injury, but we can start to control how we handle the situation.

First and foremost, it's crucial to make time for a mind-body connection. Injuries can be frustrating, but remember, healing isn't just physical — it's mental too. Take this opportunity to practise mindfulness, meditation, or even just deep breathing exercises. These activities can help manage stress, improve your mental resilience, and support your overall healing process.

Next, don't beat yourself up if your recovery takes longer than expected. Everyone's healing timeline is different, and it's important to listen to your body. Progress might be slow, but every small step forward is a victory. Celebrate those wins and be patient with yourself.

Strength training plays a pivotal role in this journey. Prior to injury, it builds resilience; during injury, it helps maintain your fitness level; and post-injury, it aids in a robust recovery. Even if you're limited in your movements, there are always ways to stay active. Consult with a healthcare professional to create a safe and effective strength training plan tailored to your needs. Knowing your body is pivotal in aiding your movement threshold.

While we're injured it can be a mental journey indeed — frustration, annoyance, and overwhelm are all key factors. Consistency will trump all. Build the "movement snacks" into your day, and your body will thank you!

Remember, this is just a chapter in your story, not the whole book. Embrace the process, focus on what you can control, and rebuild yourself with positivity and strength.

For me it's been physio once a week and religiously doing the homework. Currently doing the mobility each morning while binge watching Fast and Furious or the Golden Girls. If I don't have the time to do my whole strength session in one go, I'll break it up over two days. Get the overall weekly session in however you can!

Stay strong and keep moving forward!
