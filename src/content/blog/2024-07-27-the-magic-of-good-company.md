Hey,

I hope this finds you with a big smile and a happy heart! 😊 Today, let's dive into something close to all our hearts: the incredible power of the company we keep. Inspired by a recent catch-up with my girls — the OG crew from high school and my high-school-job decade.

Picture this: a sunny afternoon, a cosy old-school-vibe restaurant (FYI, I 100% recommend Babas in Marrickville), and the joyful laughter of your three best mates. You know that warm, fuzzy feeling? That's the magic of true friendship!

Surrounding ourselves with positive, supportive people not only boosts our mood but also fuels our personal growth.

**Mood boosters.** Have you ever noticed how your mood skyrockets after spending time with those who genuinely care about you? True friends have this uncanny ability to lift our spirits, remind us of our strengths, and bring out the best in us.

**Personal growth.** When we surround ourselves with people who are further along life's path, we open ourselves up to a world of wisdom and experience. They inspire us to dream bigger, challenge ourselves, and step out of our comfort zones.

**Project power.** Ever brainstormed an idea with friends and felt that spark of creativity? Collaborating with a supportive network can turn a simple project into a masterpiece — fresh perspectives, constructive feedback, and sometimes the gentle push we need to keep going.

**Self-perception.** Our friends and mentors help shape how we see ourselves. They reflect our best qualities, encourage us to embrace our uniqueness, and remind us that we're capable of amazing things.

So, next time you have lunch with your besties or catch up with a mentor, remember you're not just having a good time — you're nurturing your soul and setting the stage for incredible personal growth. Here's to the wonderful company we keep and the magic they bring into our lives!

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
