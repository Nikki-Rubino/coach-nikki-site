Hey,

Ever had one of those weeks where the universe feels *a little* too loud, and your brain won't stop doing laps around every idea you've ever had — plus 14 new ones at 3am? Same.

But this week I got lucky: a dear friend came to town. You know the kind — someone who just *gets you*. Zero performance required. The ones who give you the raised eyebrow of "what are you actually trying to do here?" and then hand you a gin glass or kombucha depending on your energy levels. Cue the laughs, the reality checks, and the low-key therapy that doesn't come with an invoice.

Why am I telling you this? Because whether it's life, training, or business — **we all need a soundboard**. Someone who listens *without fixing*, who challenges you *without judging*, and who makes you laugh so hard you forget you were spiralling five minutes ago.

Even the strongest humans (yes, you included) need that *circle of trust* — the people who remind you that overthinking isn't a fitness plan, who call out your self-doubt but kindly, and who hype you up when you forgot how awesome you are.

So here's your reminder: if you've been in your own head lately, send the text. Make the plan. Host the dinner (even if it's takeaway and no one wears real pants). Good conversation and good company can do wonders for your nervous system.

And if you don't have that circle yet? That's what we're building here too. Whether you walk into one of my sessions or slide into my DMs with a nervous "I'm new but curious" message, you're already halfway in. Big laughs, stronger lifts, better vibes. Community is the secret sauce.

Keep showing up. Stay Sassy,
Nikki
