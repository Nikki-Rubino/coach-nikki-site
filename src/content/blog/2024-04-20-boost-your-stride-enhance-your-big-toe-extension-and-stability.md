How did you go releasing and activating your arch last week? I'd love to know what you found most difficult.

This week is all about increasing your big toe extension. So what is big toe extension? It's the ability to bend and put pressure onto your big toe joint.

Why is it important? When you walk or run, the big toe plays an essential role in arch stabilisation during midstance and the take-off phase of the normal gait cycle. Also, think about your rear foot position when walking downstairs — if the toes can't bend and carry some load, stability is gone!

There are a handful of ways to introduce mobility and stability back into your foot, building from a beginner big toe extension, to a loaded version, to intermediate and advanced loaded toe extension. Give them a try and let me know how you go.

A simple few minutes a day can make a lot of difference over the course of a week, a month and a year.
