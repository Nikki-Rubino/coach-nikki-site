Hey,

So if you're keeping up to date, you've listed the goal and how you're emotionally connected to it, and thought of a strategy or plan on how you're going to achieve it. So what's next? We need the tactics, or actions. We need to fill in the how.

How am I going to fulfil this thing I want, or become the person I want to become? Often the process can feel so overwhelming and out of reach that we simply leave it as a wish and never get closer. So I propose some questions to help unblock the thought process:

1. **Why do I want to achieve this goal?** This should already be answered from week one. It's great to keep coming back to this meaning as your anchor point, or your driver for doing the harder things to come.

2. **What is holding me back? What obstacles am I facing?** Go deeper — list the limiting factors. Once we know what's really holding us back (time, experience, money, lack of knowledge), we can get clear on what we need to overcome them.

3. **How am I going to accomplish this goal? Is there a way I can unlock the roadblock above?** Do you need help from a coach, a course, a friend, or Google?

4. **What will be my reward?** This can be something physical or emotional. "When I get a new client, that will cover a monthly massage." "I will gain confidence to serve others once I finish this course."

James Clear makes it really simple in *Atomic Habits*: I will [action], at [time], in [location]. Going back to my handstand goal, my roadblock is my left shoulder strength. So I created two actions: I'll do my 15-minute shoulder drills at 4:30pm at Jungle Brothers on Monday, Tuesday and Friday (the days I coach in the evenings, so I'm downstairs 15 minutes earlier). And I'll practise my 60-minute handstand sessions at 1pm on Thursdays at my parents' house.

Knowing what you want, realising the roadblocks stopping you, and overcoming those blocks with a solution is paramount. Then create an action — simple is key. Go forth and see what pops up for you. I'd love to know what actions you're going to implement from this week forward.
