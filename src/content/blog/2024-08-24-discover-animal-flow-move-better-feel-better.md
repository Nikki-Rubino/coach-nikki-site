Hey,

Today I'm excited! It's been a hell of a week — who should I fire for scheduling my first webinar presentation and hosting the International Animal Flow Day event in the same week? Oh right, that's on me! As Kelly Clarkson says, "What doesn't kill you makes you stronger!"

This year marks the 4th International Animal Flow Day. I first stumbled upon Animal Flow back in July 2016 while scrolling through my PT study feed. At the time, I was knee-deep in advertising, juggling my PT certifications, and planning my escape from the ad world within 12 months. I thought, "Hey, a bodyweight-only program sounds great — I don't need to shell out cash for equipment!" But once I dived into the 16-hour, 2-day course, I realised this was something special.

The concept of mastering our movement for strength, mobility, flexibility, and endurance — all on the floor — had me hooked. It was about more than just breaking a sweat; it was about unlocking stiff joints, figuring out where my strengths and limitations were, and moving with purpose.

Back then, I was 33, living on a steady diet of bootcamp, Body Pump, spin, F45 — anything that promised high intensity, fast pace, and a healthy dose of competition. Six days a week, one hour each morning. Then I'd plant myself in a dark TV edit suite for 8, 10, 12+ hours until the job was done. I thought I was strong and healthy, but inside, my body was screaming — hip pain, shoulder pain, lower back pain. I even had elbow pain that baffled three different instructors.

Fast forward 8 years, and Animal Flow is now a crucial part of my life. It's a tool I use not just for myself but for every person I coach, whether they're 38 or 88. If you've heard the words "beast," "crab," "wave unload," "crab reach," or "side kick thru," you've had a taste of Animal Flow.

Here are 4 benefits of Quadrupedal Movement Training:

**1. Community.** As humans we need positive interactions with others; without them it can have profound effects on our health. A safe place to share stories and insights — these interactions can reduce stress, improve the immune system, and, as we age, lower the risk of dementia. Conversations keep our minds active! Last year I took my 40th birthday global, travelling to Boulder, Colorado for the Level 3 Certification workshop, then through the States, the Netherlands and the UK, meeting fellow flowists and friends finally in person rather than through a Zoom screen.

**2. Scalability & inclusivity.** Everyone from age 38 to 88 experiences elements of Animal Flow in their programming with me. It's a versatile tool for assessment, cognitive function, and strength. The system has 6 components that can be regressed as much as needed. The cognitive aspect — trust me, you have to think as you move — is especially beneficial for those with brain-degenerative conditions like dementia or Parkinson's.

**3. Strength & mobility.** Animal Flow fills in the training gaps you didn't know you had. Imagine a bodybuilder in a yoga class and a yoga instructor lifting heavy weights — both sit at opposite ends of the spectrum, but the sweet spot is right in the middle. Lifting heavy is great for bone density, heart health, and muscle, but it can limit your joint range. Yoga opens up your joints, but can you control that range with strength? We need both. Animal Flow helps you get strong and controlled through your full range of motion.

**4. Endurance and resilience.** Animal Flow isn't just physical — it challenges your mental fortitude too. The drills, the moments of losing control, using the wrong limbs, the countless "one more times." I always kick off my intro sessions with, "Don't be alarmed if, within the first 20 minutes, you forget what's left, right, and which limb is which." There's always a chuckle, but sure enough, at least three people will experience it. It develops over time — trust me, I know what it feels like to be a flailing baby giraffe. But the rewards when you start to feel progress? Absolutely priceless.

In summary, Animal Flow is my movement meditation, a complement to strength training, and an inclusive global community that makes this big wide world feel smaller. A practice backed by science and filled with knowledgeable, genuine, humble humans simply wanting to move well and stay connected.

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
