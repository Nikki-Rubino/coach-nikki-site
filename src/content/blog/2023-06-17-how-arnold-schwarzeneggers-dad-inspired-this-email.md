Hey,

It's been a while, I do realise. It was Arnold Schwarzenegger's dad that inspired me to get back into the Sunday sessions (I highly recommend watching *Arnold* on Netflix).

I'm back, and have been working on some exciting upcoming projects, along with being overseas for a month for my Animal Flow Level 3 workshop in Colorado, then visiting international flow instructors in real life through the States, the Netherlands and the UK! It's been eye-opening visiting so many other gyms and instructors — and turning 40 since we last interacted.

I'll be using these Sunday set-up sessions to share quick actions you can take into your week to spread joy and education. Welcome to the new format, where I'll share a little weekly insight along with good vibes in the form of one weekly mantra, one morning dance tune, and one educational read.

If there's anything you specifically want to hear about as we enter the cooler months and movement motivation may be dwindling, just let me know by replying.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
