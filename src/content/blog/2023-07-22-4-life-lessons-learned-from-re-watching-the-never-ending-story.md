Hey,

How did an impromptu night out at Hot Dub Time Machine prompt me to rewatch *The NeverEnding Story* at age 40?

Hands up if you're a fellow 80s baby scarred forever by the Atreyu and Artax swamp scene.

Why was I compelled to watch it? The banger track played a snippet at the gig, with the film of Atreyu and Falkor glimpsed on the big screen. In my light-up shoes I sang my little off-tune heart out to "NeverEnding Story". The following evening, cricket was on (which I cannot stand), so I went in search of where I could watch it while doing some painting. As I sat and listened, paintbrush in hand, I was not prepared for the life lessons to hit me so hard.

**1. The importance of community.** The whole of Fantasia was on a quest for the same thing: to save it. To band together to stop the Nothing and return to life as they knew it. A whole mixture of creatures great and small, with various strengths and weaknesses. *Key takeaway:* having people around you with the same values can serve your life's purpose — a chance for encouragement and to share in each other's strengths to accomplish a valued outcome, no matter what the differences are.

**2. We can't help everyone.** Here it is, the scene that scarred so many 80s babies. Atreyu and his horse Artax trudge through the swamp of sadness. What hit home is that Artax simply did not want to be saved — he didn't even budge or try to help Atreyu help him out of that swamp. He was done. Paired with the Rockbiter, so sad that his "strong hands" couldn't save his friends. *Key takeaway:* people are going to enter and leave our lives. We can do all we can to help them through, but if they don't want to be saved, there's nothing we can do. Mistakes, accidents and unknown incidents will happen; we can't carry that pain with us, and need to keep trudging forward, no matter what that looks like.

**3. Self-doubt is a killer.** Atreyu has to pass the sphinx gates — two sphinxes facing each other with killer laser beams if they open their eyes. When do they open their eyes? When the passer-by begins to doubt themselves. A fancy knight in full armour on a horse thinks positive, when BOOM — eyes open, lasers fired, knight burnt to a crisp. Atreyu takes himself down to the gates, no weapons, no horse, just him and the confident thought that he'll get through for the greater mission. Spoiler: he makes it. *Key takeaway:* back yourself, no matter how scary the thing in front of you. Take ownership from within — no fancy armour required.

**4. Who is your Falkor?** Falkor the luck dragon is wise, optimistic, dignified and friendly, giving advice to others who have lost hope. *Key takeaway:* who are the people around you whose attitude comes purely from the heart? Who do you value, trust and treasure in your circle? Do they know they're your Falkor? Maybe you can tell them. Or maybe you can think of someone you might be a Falkor to yourself.

So there we have it — rewatching old favourites and learning life lessons from a "kids" film. Does anyone else resort to watching old things they've seen before when life is a little unknown, searching for comfort in the things you already know the outcome of? Or is that just me?
