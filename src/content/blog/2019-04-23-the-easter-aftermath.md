Hey,

Move to see what your body can do — not as punishment for what you've done!

Ok, so the long weekend is over. You were out of your usual routine and things may have been a little different on the movement and nutrition side of things. And that's okay! I've heard a few times this morning: "I need to thrash myself this week to catch up", "I ate so many Easter eggs it's going to take forever to burn them off", "I did nothing over the break, I need to work double to make up for it."

Stop. Can we please stop using exercise as a punishment for what we've done? And can we please ditch the damn guilt! Yes, you may have consumed more over the weekend, but more than likely you were in an enjoyable environment with friends or family, interacting and embracing the moment. It's done — you had fun and lived life. Is that such a bad thing? And if you did nothing, good on you for giving yourself a break. Recharging is one of the best things we can do for our mental and physical health.

Today is another day to resume your path towards your goal — whatever that may be. Speaking of goals, do you have one that's not aesthetically based? I ask all my clients: what is it you want your body to be able to do, rather than be? If we have a goal we're actually moving towards, it can be so satisfying when we do something we thought was impossible. The side benefits: we get stronger, our bodies adapt and change, we reduce stress, we sleep better, and we start to make better choices. It's simply the by-product of consistent work towards something that actually drives you.

I understand we all want to look good, but is that the be-all and end-all? I believe not. You can look awesome and still not feel good about yourself. And who actually defines what "looking good" is anyway?

So my question for you is: how do you want to feel? What is stopping you from feeling that now? And what is one habit you can change or monitor to bridge that gap?

Hope you all enjoyed the break and did whatever makes your heart dance!

Until next time,
Nikki
