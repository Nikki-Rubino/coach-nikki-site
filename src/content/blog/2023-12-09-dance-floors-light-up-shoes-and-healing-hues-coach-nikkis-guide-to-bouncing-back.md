Hey,

Hope this email finds you in good spirits despite the not-so-good things floating in the air!

As I sit down to write this, I can't help but reflect on the mix of high energy and lethargy that made this week quite the rollercoaster ride.

First things first — the blast from the past, the 90s bash last weekend! Can we just agree that the 90s were the epitome of fun? Colourful clothes, banging tunes, and good vibes all around — what's not to love? I even confess to being a 40-year-old with light-up shoes, ready to hit the dance floor at any given opportunity!

Now, the aftermath of all that energy hit me hard on Sunday. Leaving the gym at 2am, back for cleaning at 11 — it was a wild ride. Then came Tuesday morning, full of joy and laughter filming our annual Christmas lip-syncing video. However, by Tuesday night I had class attendees speaking for me, because my throat decided to take a vacation. Not COVID, thankfully, as I was not mentally prepared to cancel the Coach Nikki Christmas shenanigans booked for that night — but definitely a striking reminder that health is indeed wealth!

Which brings me to an important topic: what to do with your training when you're under the weather. Here are some nuggets of wisdom to consider:

**Prioritise rest.** Your body's a warrior fighting off something, so give it a break. Stress won't help the healing process.

**Joint nourishment.** Combat that sedentary feeling with some gentle stretches. Hip flexor and chest stretches can work wonders.

**Lighten your load.** Peel back on the weights. A session or two of bodyweight exercises can keep you moving without overexerting.

**Fresh air strolls.** Sometimes a simple walk in the fresh air is the best medicine. Your body craves movement, and it doesn't have to be an intense workout.

**Fluids are your friends.** That's right, flush those germs away! Stay hydrated — your joints, tissues and head will thank you.

Remember, your health is paramount, not just for yourself but for those who depend on you. Training builds resilience, ensuring that when sickness strikes you can bounce back quicker than ever!

Take care and stay healthy!

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
