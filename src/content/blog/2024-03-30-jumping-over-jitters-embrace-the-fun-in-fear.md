Hey,

This week has been a rollercoaster of emotions for me, especially with the looming deadline for a final presentation from a course I've been attending for the past six months.

Public speaking has always been a major fear of mine, stemming from a rather traumatic experience back in high school. I vividly remember a teacher tying my hands behind my back during a presentation, insisting that gesturing was unprofessional. As an expressive Italian, this was incredibly challenging, and it left a lasting mark on how I approach speaking engagements.

Despite this fear, I've been determined to overcome it. So I enrolled in a six-month speakers' course, gradually pushing myself out of my comfort zone with each passing month. And now, as the final assessment approaches — where I have to create and film a presentation on a topic of my choice — the pressure is on. There's even the possibility of using it as an application to speak at an upcoming event, the Fuel Women in Fitness Business Summit. Talk about stepping out of my comfort zone!

With the deadline fast approaching, I've been pulling out all the stops to get everything done over the Easter weekend. Procrastination has been my constant companion — whether it's cleaning or snacking — but once I finally dive in, it's never as bad as I anticipated.

So here's to facing our fears, embracing vulnerability, and taking leaps into the unknown. Wish me luck as I tackle this final hurdle! I encourage you to step into the unknown with courage. Embracing the fear, one baby step at a time — because growth is born from the dance between discomfort and determination.

A little something to sit with this week: run towards the roar. Did you know that a pride of lions positions the older lions, who can't run, on one side, and the younger agile ones on the perimeter? When hunting, they use the older lions to do the loud roar, which makes the prey run in the opposite direction — right into the young agile crew. The takeaway: head towards the things that scare you, and you may actually find safety.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
