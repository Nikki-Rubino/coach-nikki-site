Hey,

This week I want to give a huge shout out to a Ninja who reached Level 70 in this game of life!

Five years ago, her birthday was spent in a hospital bed. This week, she celebrates turning 70 — strong, mobile, and pain-free. She's proof that age is not the limiter — your approach is.

Since training commenced in 2023, she's:

- Rebuilt her strength for daily life
- Travelled overseas without knee pain
- Shown up, rain or shine, no matter how the body or mind felt that day, and worked hard

This kind of transformation doesn't happen overnight — it's the power of consistency. Strength training isn't just about lifting weights; it's about lifting your quality of life.

Why is strength training a non-negotiable as we age? Because muscle loss (sarcopenia) starts in our 30s and accelerates if we don't actively fight it. Studies show that strength training 2–3 times per week can reduce the risk of falls, keep joints healthy, and even improve mental resilience. It's not about "anti-aging" — it's about pro-living.

One of my favourite quotes is: "You don't stop playing because you grow old; you grow old because you stop playing." — George Bernard Shaw

Anne is out here playing, moving, and thriving at 70. If that's not a reason to keep showing up, I don't know what is. Here's to lifting, laughing, and ageing disgracefully. 💪🔥

So, if you're still on the fence about making movement a priority, ask yourself: where do you want to be in 5 years? Stronger, more capable, and free to do what you love? Or stuck, stiff, and in pain? Your future self is counting on you.

Keep moving and stay Sassy,
Nikki
