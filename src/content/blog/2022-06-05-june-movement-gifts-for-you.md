Hey,

With the Arctic winter blast in full force this week, welcome to winter! It was also my birthday week, so here I am at level 39 in life.

Being a celebratory stage, I want to share with you some of my favourite forms of movement. Why? Knowing my tribe vibe and what lights me up helps me through what is the toughest month of the year. You may or may not know that I lost my big sister suddenly in June, 12 years ago on June 19th — my birthday was the last time I remember seeing her. It's taken me a long time and I'm still healing. I don't say this to bring anyone down for a Sunday; I merely want to highlight that everyone has a story, and you may not know what someone is going through. So be kind.

Sharing knowledge, helping people, conscious movement, and surrounding myself with people who light me up are what get me through the days I don't want to deal with the world. So let me share a small insight into the Animal Flow practice. With the weather cooling, these movement snacks throughout your day, in small doses, will warm you up, get your brain and body working together, and can be done anywhere. This week is about wrist preparation and activations — use them as a mini-break from your desk, a warm-up to prime the body before training, or simply a check-in to see how connected you are with your body at that moment:

- **Wrist release** — unwind tight wrists and hands from your keyboard and prime them for movement.
- **Wrist prep and strength** — do you experience pain in push-ups or hand-balance practice? These regressions and prep moves help your wrist joint health.
- **Activation: beast** — perfect for core conditioning, prepping the shoulders, core, glutes, quads and feet for training.
- **Activation: crab** — perfect to unwind desk-locked posture and strengthen the posterior chain: pretty much everything from the base of your neck, down your spine, glutes, hamstrings and calves.

I'd love to know which ones you try, or what resonates with you. If you try something and it's a little too intense, reply and let me know — there are plenty of other options to start with.

Until next week,
Nikki
