Hey,

As we journey together through the wonderful world of fitness, I want to touch on something that can make all the difference in how we approach our workouts and, ultimately, our lives.

It's all too easy to get caught up in the mirror, focusing on what our bodies look like.

I've witnessed first hand someone try to reach their "aesthetic goal" by any means possible. While their body looked a certain way, it did not mean they were healthy by any means. Striving for aesthetics only meant that once the body got into trouble — with crippling arthritis at a young age and movement restricted — the aesthetics changed, and it took a toll mentally.

While aesthetics are a part of the fitness journey, there's so much more magic in training to discover what our bodies can do.

1. **Strength over scale:** imagine lifting a weight you never thought possible, or running that extra kilometre without gasping for air. When we train for performance, we tap into the joy of seeing our strength, endurance and agility improve. It's like unlocking new levels in a video game — each accomplishment fuels your motivation to reach the next.

2. **Mental resilience:** training for capability rather than appearance builds mental toughness. When you push through that final rep or nail that challenging Animal Flow pose, you're not just strengthening your body — you're fortifying your mind. This resilience spills over into other areas of life.

3. **Fun factor:** focusing on what your body can do turns exercise into an exciting adventure. Whether it's mastering a new dance routine, conquering a tough hike, or smashing a personal best in the gym, the variety and excitement keep you engaged and eager for more.

4. **Body positivity:** when the emphasis shifts from looks to abilities, you develop a healthier relationship with your body. You start appreciating it for the amazing things it can achieve rather than criticising it for perceived flaws. This shift fosters a more positive self-image and boosts overall happiness.

5. **Lifelong fitness:** training for functionality encourages sustainable habits. It's not about crash diets or extreme measures, but about consistent, enjoyable movement. This mindset ensures that fitness remains a lifelong journey rather than a fleeting phase.

6. **Community connection:** engaging in performance-based training often means joining communities with similar goals. Whether it's a running club, an Animal Flow session, or a dance class, these groups provide support, camaraderie, and an extra dose of fun. You're not just working out — you're part of a team.

**Take the challenge!** This week, I challenge you to set a performance-based goal. It could be anything from battling a hill, running a 5K, perfecting an Animal Flow pose, or even learning a new sport. Focus on the process and the little victories along the way.

Remember, your body is an incredible machine capable of amazing feats. Let's celebrate what it can do, and the aesthetics will follow naturally.

Stay awesome and keep pushing your limits! I'd love to hear about the goals you're setting and the progress you're making. Drop me a reply, and let's cheer each other on.

Until next week,
Nikki
