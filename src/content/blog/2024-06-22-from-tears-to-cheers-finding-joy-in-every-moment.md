Hey,

I hope this email finds you in good spirits, sipping your favourite coffee (or tea, or maybe something stronger — it's been that kind of week, right?). As we navigate through the ebbs and flows of life, I wanted to drop a note of encouragement and a sprinkle of fun to brighten your day.

Let's be real: life can sometimes feel like a wild rollercoaster at an amusement park. This week was the anniversary of suddenly losing my big sister. Although it's been 14 years, on this day a flood of memories, emotions and paralysing moments wash over me. I feel fragile and vulnerable, and I'll need to fight back tears and a shaking body as memories flood back out of nowhere. In life there are moments of exhilarating highs and, inevitably, some gut-wrenching lows. It's those tough days — the ones where memories of lost loved ones weigh heavy on our hearts — that truly test our resilience.

But here's the thing: just like every rollercoaster, the ride eventually levels out. And while we can't erase the sadness, we can use it as a springboard to bounce back even stronger. Think of it as the emotional equivalent of a trampoline — no matter how hard we fall, we rise with even more energy and spirit.

This week, let's focus on the little joys that make life beautiful. Here are a few ideas to get us started:

- **Random acts of kindness:** surprise someone with a compliment or a small gesture — offer to buy a cup of coffee if you're off to the cafe. It's amazing how much joy a simple act of kindness can bring to both the giver and the receiver.
- **Memory lane marathon:** take a trip down memory lane, but with a twist. Celebrate the happy moments shared with those we've lost. Revisit old photos, tell funny stories, and let the nostalgia bring a smile instead of a tear. (As I write this, I'm in the middle of binge watching the Fast and Furious franchise — currently on number six since Wednesday. It's great to pass the time doing back rehab and food prep.)
- **Dance like nobody's watching:** seriously, crank up your favourite tunes and have a solo dance party. It's scientifically proven (by me) that dancing like a goofball releases endorphins. To quote Sandra Bullock: "The rule is you have to dance a little bit in the morning before you leave the house, because it changes the way you walk out into the world."
- **Nature therapy:** step outside and soak in the beauty of nature. Whether it's a walk in the park, a hike in the hills, or simply sitting in your garden, let the fresh air and sunshine work their magic. With the weather as it is, even just staring out the window or stepping onto a balcony can do wonders.
- **Gratitude journal:** start or revisit a gratitude journal. Jot down three things you're thankful for each day. It doesn't have to be the grand things — simply being grateful for a roof over your head, food on the table, or a meme sent as an inside joke between you and another human.

Remember, it's okay to have hard days. It's all part of the human experience. But with each challenging moment, we have the opportunity to grow, to learn, and to appreciate the brighter days even more.

So, as we move through this week, let's embrace the full spectrum of our emotions. Let's laugh a little louder, love a little harder, and find joy in the journey — because life, with all its ups and downs, is a ride worth celebrating.

Sending you a big virtual hug and a high-five for making it through another week. Keep shining, you amazing human, and remember to stay Sassy!

Until next week,
Nikki
