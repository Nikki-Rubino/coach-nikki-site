Hi Friend,

Have you heard of the teapot analogy?

> You can't give to others if you are feeling empty yourself.

But what if we flip that and reframe it — we need to nourish ourselves and fill our own cup *so* we can be there for others. Or as Harry Styles puts it: fill yourself up and let others fall in love with the overflow.

If you're tired, overwhelmed, under-stimulated, over-stimulated, or run down, how are you going to fulfil your own responsibilities and be there for the ones who need you?

- Nourishing your **body** makes you physically feel better and ready to tackle what's ahead.
- Nourishing your **mind** can calm an overactive head and unblock that stuck feeling.
- Nourishing your **inner self** can open the floodgates of gratitude, boost your mood, and help you get to know yourself.

Here are some free and inexpensive ways to get short, sweet little teapot refills into your week.

**Ideas to nourish your body**

- Go for a walk
- Get barefoot outside on different textures — grass, wood, dirt, or even pebbles if you can handle it
- Drink water first thing in the morning
- Simply move your body in a way you enjoy for 5 minutes — stretches, yoga, Animal Flow, a dance party for one
- Hang, literally — from an overhead bar or anything that supports your bodyweight. It decompresses the spine, builds grip strength, and improves shoulder health

**Ideas to nourish your mind**

- Listen to an interview with someone you admire
- Watch a different genre of movie than your usual
- Read or listen to something for fun
- Learn a new skill
- Plan a short getaway or your next holiday
- Catch up with a friend
- Journal
- Spend time with those who inspire and encourage you

**Ideas to nourish your inner self (soul)**

- Play with your pet (or someone else's)
- Meditate for 5 minutes (I don't find sitting still easy — a short guided meditation helped me start the habit)
- Smile at a stranger
- Praise someone you admire and tell them why
- Give someone who needs it a random note of encouragement
- Write down 3–5 things you're grateful for each day
- Give yourself a big goal that seems a little scary to start

The more we know ourselves, the more we realise what we need to recharge and re-energise — giving us the full capacity to tackle life's demands. I'd love to hear how you refill your teapot.

Until next week, Stay Sassy,
Nikki
