Hey,

It's been two weeks since we flung open the doors to the new studio, and what a ride it's been! Like any big change, there's been a bit of adjusting, but honestly, it's been an absolute ripper seeing everyone rock up and not let a bit of holiday downtime throw them off their game.

It's been so inspiring to see the energy and determination you've brought back into your routine. But let's be real — not every session feels like a barrel of laughs, right? Some days, it's hard yakka. And that's totally fine. What makes the difference is reconnecting with your why on those tougher days.

**Why strength training is an investment**

Ever heard this beauty? "If you think being healthy is expensive, try being sick." Too right! Every squat, push-up, and lunge is like putting dosh in your future health piggy bank. Strength training isn't just about today; it's about making sure future-you can still do the things you love. Whether it's hiking up a bush track, dancing at your mate's 70th, or staying put in your own home for as long as possible, what you do now sets you up for all that good stuff.

This week, I had a cracking chat with one of our ninjas who's been battling a bit of hip pain. She's been back and forth to the physio, and they've given her a list of seven exercises to tackle. Overwhelming much? So, we broke it down:

- The plan: three mornings a week, 20 minutes per session.
- The setup: keep the band next to her bedside table as a cheeky reminder.
- The why: without the ability to handle her stairs, she'd have to consider moving house. That's a massive life change she's determined to avoid.

Now she's got a doable plan that makes sense and hits her right in the feels. Talk about taking charge!

**Find your why**

So, what's your why? Why are you showing up to train? Maybe it's to keep up with your grandkids when they're running amok, stay limber for your weekend adventures, or dodge those nasty health problems that slow you down. Whatever it is, dig deep and really picture it. Then break it into bite-sized, achievable steps. Just like my client's plan, small changes build into massive wins over time.

It's been a blast seeing everyone's grit and good humour these past couple of weeks. Life throws its curveballs, but you lot are smashing it by prioritising your health and getting stuck in. Keep at it — even when it's not easy — because every little effort adds up to something pretty bloody brilliant. Here's to another week of progress: one step, one session, one cheeky grin at a time.

Cheers and stay Sassy,
Nikki
