Hey,

The world can feel like quite a negative place at times. There's so much in our lives that can put us in a stress spiral — pandemics, the rising cost of day-to-day life, work demands, family demands, trying to balance everything you need to do. And that's just the external factors. There are also days when your emotions simply get the better of you for a plethora of reasons.

You may have heard the benefits of a gratitude diary or journal — it's great for appreciating what you have right now. But have you ever thought about the people you're thankful for in your life?

**Beginner journaling tip #2 — your hype team.** Who are the people in your life right now who genuinely hype you up and put a smile on your face when you see them or hear from them? They give you energy, stay optimistic about your ideas, and make you feel happy and appreciated.

1. Write each of these people down.
2. Write why you think each is a positive influence in your life right now.

Write as much or as little as you like, and see where your mind takes you in gratitude for this person. You can choose whether or not to share the writing with them, or simply keep a stocktake of the various people who come into your life along the way.

Try it for one week: set a timer, choose one person per day, and see what happens in a mental shift. You'll also have just created your go-to list of hype team members to contact on the days you don't feel so flash and need a coffee date, a walk, or simply a phone call or text.

Go forth and realise your hype tribe!

Until next week,
