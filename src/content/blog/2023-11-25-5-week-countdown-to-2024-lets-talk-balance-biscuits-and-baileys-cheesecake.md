Hey,

Can you believe it? We've got a mere five weeks left until we bid farewell to 2023. Say what?!

Now, onto some interesting observations from the past couple of weeks. I've been noticing a trend during our sessions. When I pop the question, "So how's the body feeling today?", many have been responding with, "Tired. It's strange — I haven't done a whole lot extra, but I'm feeling tired."

So I put on my detective hat and started asking a few more questions. Any work deadlines looming? Are you hosting the ultimate holiday bash? Planning an escape to a tropical paradise? Has your social calendar suddenly turned into a game of Tetris?

Ah, the end-of-year hustle is real! We're all trying to tie up loose ends, plan epic gatherings, attend work parties, shop for presents, cook up a storm, and maybe even squeeze in some travel. The mental load is flexing its muscles.

Speaking of mental gymnastics, have you ever been so lost in thought that you stubbed your toe or walked into a wall? Guilty as charged, right? When our minds are in overdrive, there's a tendency to lose touch with what's happening in our bodies. But fear not — here are three tips to help you navigate this wild ride safely and effectively:

**Mindfulness and breath.** Take a breather, literally! Whether it's five minutes of mindfulness with apps like Headspace or Calm, or a guided meditation playlist, let's manage that stress and sharpen our focus.

**Write things down.** It's time for a brain dump! Vent, jot down your to-do list, or track your lifting progress on paper. Relieve that mental storage load by transferring it from your mind to good old paper.

**Regress to progress.** We all want to hit personal bests, but let's be realistic. If your body isn't up for the challenge, there's no shame in dialling it back a notch. Progress isn't always about pushing harder; sometimes it's about finding balance.

As a side note, I had a hilarious encounter with my folks three weeks ago. I opened the cupboard, spotted a specific type of biscuit, and declared, "Well, Mum's already got Christmas on her mind!" Those biscuits are the secret weapon for the infamous Baileys cheesecake. Plans were in motion, and the pre-planning had begun.

So here's to balancing the mental load, enjoying the holidays, and not getting injured while daydreaming about Baileys cheesecake.

Cheers to a fantastic end to 2023. Let's go!

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
