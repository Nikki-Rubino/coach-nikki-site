Hey,

So where did you venture outside last week? I hope these simple little tips are bringing some positive vibes through your month of May. As we inch closer to winter, having recognised some tools that help shift the mood can be really beneficial. This week is something a little different — still positive, but potentially something you may not have thought of.

**Mood boosting tip #7 — learn something.** This can be anything new! A movement skill, a language, an instrument, salsa dancing, reading up on a topic of interest, or listening to a podcast. When we learn, we challenge our brain — to think about a different perspective, or to physically learn something it hasn't been exposed to. It can also give a sense of accomplishment once something is unlocked.

**Mood boosting tip #8 — share knowledge.** This can be a continuation of something you learned and passed on, or simply something you know that could benefit someone else. You don't have to give away all the prize knowledge nuggets you've spent time and money acquiring — it could be as complex or as simple as you like. We humans are complex, but we thrive on community, and sharing is part of building connections. We all have skillsets and strengths; what you find easy and a no-brainer may be different for someone else. You never know what skillset you'll need one day, and sharing yours can expose you to someone whose skillset you may need in future.

Go forth, learn and share! I'm always open to learning something, so any knowledge nuggets you want to share, hit me up.

Until next week,
Nikki
