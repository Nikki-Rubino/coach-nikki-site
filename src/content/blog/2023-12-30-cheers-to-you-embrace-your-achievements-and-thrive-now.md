Hey,

As we approach the end of the year, it's natural for many of us to reflect on the past and look ahead. The familiar phrase "New Year, New Me" often dominates our thoughts, urging us to set resolutions and create grand plans for self-improvement. However, in the midst of these aspirations, it's crucial to take a moment to appreciate and celebrate the incredible journey you've embarked upon this year.

I want to remind you that each step you've taken, every challenge you've overcome, and all the small victories you've experienced are worth acknowledging. Whether you have a tangible list of accomplishments or you're simply proud of surviving the rollercoaster of life, you are enough just as you are. Your journey is uniquely yours, and it deserves to be recognised and celebrated.

Take a moment to reflect on the hurdles you've conquered, the skills you've developed, and the resilience you've shown in the face of adversity. Your growth may not always be quantifiable, and that's perfectly okay. It's about the lessons learned, the strength gained, and the person you've become in the process. Sometimes it's the internal victories — the moments of self-discovery, self-acceptance and personal growth — that are the most profound.

As we step into the new year, let's not forget to honour the present and embrace the incredible person you are today. You are a work in progress, constantly evolving and growing. Celebrate your journey, no matter how unconventional or unpredictable it may be.

Make a list of the top five to 10 things you are proud of this year — either keep it to yourself or shout it from the rooftops. Here are my 2023 top five achievements, in no particular order:

- Attending my first concert solo (Harry Styles)
- Nine-year marriage anniversary
- A month of solo travel through the States, Netherlands and UK for my Animal Flow Level 3 certification workshop, and meeting fellow AF instructors in person
- Growing my first ever veggie patch
- Getting upside down on the ceiling rings by myself for a half "skin the cat"

So off you toddle — go and give yourself a little list and a high five in the mirror for whatever you're damn proud of! Celebrate your achievements, and cheers to a new year filled with even more growth, fulfilment, joy and laughter.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
