Hey,

With the cold onset and grey clouds through winter, it can all feel a little doom and gloom — whether it's nutrition, movement, mindset, projects, or day-to-day tasks. Things can simply feel harder during this time.

When we're stressed, our cortisol levels rise. Cortisol triggers our emergency, fight-or-flight response, and too much of it increases our chances of falling into a depressed or anxious state. If you encounter a setback, or are feeling sad, frustrated or jealous, try this four-step morning routine to bring yourself back to a more positive place.

Ideally do it first thing in the morning, while brushing your teeth or the first time you glance in the mirror. It doesn't matter how messed up your hair is — give the below a try, doing all four or taking what resonates with you:

1. **Smile** — a big cheesy one, who cares if your teeth aren't clean yet. Hold it for as long as you can. If you wake up really grumpy or anxious, try holding for a split second, then return to a grumpy face and keep switching for about 60 seconds.

2. **High five** — at the mirror, high five yourself, give yourself a thumbs up, a rock-out hand, whatever you deem a positive-vibe gesture.

3. **Give yourself a small compliment** — say something that resonates with you: "I'm unstoppable", "You got this", "Slay the day", "You're da bomb", "Hell yeah, go you". Whatever it is, say it out loud, and say it a few times!

4. **12 seconds of positive vibes** — sit with a feel-good moment, memory, experience or image. A person, a beloved pet, a great experience — a moment that simply made you feel good.

Simply try it for a week! I'd love to know what gets rewired for you. If you can put a positive spin on the start of your day, you're equipping yourself with the tools to problem-solve or conquer any hurdles that pop up. (For a deeper insight, watch the short TED talk "Hardwiring Happiness" by Dr Rick Hanson.)

Go forth and slay your day!

Until next week,
