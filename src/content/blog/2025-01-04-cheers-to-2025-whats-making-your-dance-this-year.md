Happy New Year!

So here we are: a new year, a clean slate, and 360 days of unknown adventures, experiences to ponder, and countless memories to create!

Whether you welcomed 2025 partying in a crowd, dancing in your living room to Robbie Williams on TV, or catching up on beauty sleep after the 9pm fireworks, the new year is officially here! (Care to guess which one I chose?)

This week, I'm turning the question to YOU: what excites you about 2025? New job prospects? Family additions on the way? Travel plans or new hobbies? Unlocking a skill or forming habits that serve you better? Big or small, I'd love to hear what's making your heart dance this year.

**What's making my heart race in 2025?**

Well, I'm excitedly terrified to kick off the year working from a brand-new co-shared space. If I'm honest, the unknown scares me. My brain loves to play the "what if" game, always starting with the negatives. But here we are — Tima and I have poured time, money, cleaning hours, shopping trips, and plenty of schedule chats into making this space a reality. And now it's time to bring it to life.

For the first time, I'm working completely under the "Coach Nikki" banner. Since leaving advertising, I've always worked under someone else's business. You might know me from "BeachFit" outdoor bootcamps, science-based hypertrophy training at "Physique Transformations" (I'll never forget those park skinfold calipers), the fight-lift-move ethos of JB, or Animal Flow sessions where bodyweight is the ultimate tool. Each chapter shaped my coaching style, giving me a diverse toolbox and a deep appreciation for what makes people strong — physically, mentally, and emotionally. Now, I'm blending everything I've learned to create my own community of strong, educated, ready-for-life humans at any stage of life.

**What's happening in the new space?**

- 1:1 coaching: starting with a full-body assessment of strength and mobility benchmarks, then personalised six-week programming cycles tailored to your goals and needs.
- Sassy Seniors classes: twice-weekly sessions focused on strength, balance, and cognitive function in a supportive, small-group setting — for those who may be intimidated by a gym session but need the benefits of what training offers.
- Animal Flow opportunities: from complete beginners to seasoned practitioners, I'm expanding offerings. And for aspiring instructors feeling like a confused baby giraffe (been there, done that), there's support for you too!
- Monthly workshops & seminars: for those who can't make regular classes, these weekend opportunities will keep you learning and moving — and showcase the other amazing coaches in my network.

Cheers to 2025! Let's make it a year of strength, joy, and badassery. Remember — keep moving to keep grooving!

Take care, stay Sassy, and let's ring in the new year together 💛
Nikki
