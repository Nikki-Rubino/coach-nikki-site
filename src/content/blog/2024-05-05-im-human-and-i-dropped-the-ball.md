Hey,

In a world where AI envelops us, I embrace my humanity — flaws and all.

In a week packed with events, I'll confess: I fumbled on Sunday's email. Amid preparing for a speech at the Fuel Summit titled "Redefining Ageing: Empowering Seniors with Sass", my mental bandwidth dwindled. I thought I could tackle it Saturday, but reality had me trading power pants for pyjamas.

This week, I delved into the intricacies of fear and our nervous system. Public speaking? Count me among the 72 to 75% who dread it! Fear isn't just a feeling; it's primal, triggering our brain's panic mode for survival. Whether it's a spider or a speech, our bodies react the same.

For weeks, the speech loomed over me, anxiety gripping my every move. Stress tightened my muscles, constricted my breath, and clouded my mind. Yet, amidst the terror, I pushed through.

Trembling legs, shallow breaths — I faced the stage. And despite the fear, I sparked vital conversations on senior strength training.

In a world where AI dominates, I embrace my humanity, imperfections and all, striving to conquer fears and spark meaningful conversations.

(A win worth celebrating: my first ever presentation, done — in a room full of fitness professionals, business owners, and Papa Bear Beano, aka my dad.)

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
