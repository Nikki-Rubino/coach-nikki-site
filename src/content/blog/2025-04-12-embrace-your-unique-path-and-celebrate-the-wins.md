Hey,

Ever feel like you're on a unique path, a little off the mainstream track? You're not alone, and that's something to celebrate! 🌟

I recently had the joy of hosting an Animal Flow workshop alongside a dear friend and mentor who's been pushing me out of my comfort zones since 2016. It was a proud moment to have him in a space I call my movement home — not just for me, but for others too. To create a social and movement hub for the practice I've loved and incorporated for 9 years. Now not only for my own clients but for other fitness and health professionals too. A safe space to learn, play and grow, where nobody gets left behind no matter how you like to learn.

After a huge week, I was excited to unwind at a dance event. While the company was fantastic, the atmosphere wasn't quite what I expected. It led to some interesting conversations about expectations and experiences, especially when they don't align with what we anticipate — even a potential re-do of the event at the studio with all of the things we had originally been excited for. That age-old saying: if you want something done right, you have to do it yourself!

This week has been an eye-opener, reminding me that it's perfectly okay to be your unique self and attract those on the same wavelength. The inner voice was LOUD this week, and I'm forever grateful for the handful of people I can reach out to — no judgements, just a listening ear and the voice of reason when I need it myself.

Transitioning into the studio has given me the freedom to create a space that resonates with who I am and what I can offer to the world. I'll be first to admit, I'm a little empathetic weirdo. I'm not mainstream and get bored very easily with "normal". I'll always be the first to say you do you, boo! And if that means wearing light-up shoes on a dance floor at level 41 in life, then so be it.

Now, I turn to you, my fellow quirks! What do you want more of in this realm of health, fitness and mindset? What do you struggle with, or what would you love me to share in these musings or at the studio? I have ideas, but I want to know what resonates with you.

Keep embracing your unique path and stay Sassy,
Nikki
