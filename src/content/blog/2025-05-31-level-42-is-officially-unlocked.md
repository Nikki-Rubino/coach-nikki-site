Hey,

Today I've officially levelled up to 42 in this wild, unpredictable, hilarious, humbling game of life.

And if I'm honest, it always feels a bit surreal. There's a deep sense of gratitude that I've made it here, continuing to grow, move, and be surrounded by incredible humans. But it's also a time that carries weight.

You see, my big sis didn't get to make it past level 30. And with her anniversary falling in June too, it brings up all the feels. That double-edged sword of celebration and reflection.

But maybe that's the whole point of levelling up. It's not just cake and candles (although I fully support both). It's perspective. It's remembering who we are, who we've lost, and who we're becoming. It's a privilege — not guaranteed — to be here, to keep playing, learning, loving, messing up, starting again, and hopefully making it all count.

So today, I'm celebrating not just the number. I'm celebrating the messy, beautiful, unfiltered gift of being alive. Of still having the chance to show up, to flow, to love hard, and to keep moving forward with purpose and a cheeky grin with a side of sass.

I'll be marking this level-up with a very *me* kind of weekend — heading to Glow Zoo, playing dance games, escaping from an escape room (hopefully), and eating something delicious with good humans.

As the saying goes: *"You don't stop dancing because you grow old — you grow old because you stop dancing."* And I've got no plans to age gracefully when I can age with neon, sass, and sweat on my brow.

And that brings me to this question: what movements do you hope to be able to do for the remaining levels of your life? For me? I want to always be able to shuffle and dance my way into each morning. Even if the moves are slower or wobblier — I just want to *move* with a sense of joy and a smile on my face.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
