Hey,

Do you ever have those weeks that are just completely out of routine, where you feel like nothing gets done and you have no idea what day it is?

Guilty — I had one of them this week indeed! Nothing about this week was part of my usual routine. With in-laws in town, new client sessions, overseas friends in Sydney, and being asked to become a godparent (with the ceremony at the end of the week), it felt like there was disruption coming from everywhere. Quick changes, pivots and decisions needed to be made that left this overthinking brain in a constant state of overwhelm — anxious and frazzled.

Life will do that sometimes, throw you some curveballs. I know I don't deal well with things sprung on me; I'm very much a creature of habit and need a little prep time to gather thoughts and get a plan of attack together. But that can't always be the case. Early in the week I had to learn to simply let go of the pressure I'd put on myself to get certain things done, and prioritise only what was necessary.

On the flip side, having a break in routine meant creating memories with others, a different perspective on what to prioritise in the time available to me, and actually spending some time in non-activewear clothes. I won't lie — the disruption left me so overwhelmed that my bedroom looked like the wardrobe had exploded when I was trying to find something suitable to wear to a christening. As ridiculous as that may seem, I've lived in activewear and jeans for the last seven years; I don't even know what "normal" clothes are anymore, or if any of my own stuff still fits me. (Then I felt out of place wearing black, as I don't own anything white — I literally own animal print and fluoro outside of Animal Flow and Jungle Brothers branding.)

So I'll leave you with this for the next time you feel a little frazzled, from Lao Tzu: "If you are depressed you are living in the past. If you are anxious you are living in the future. If you are at peace you are living in the present."

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
