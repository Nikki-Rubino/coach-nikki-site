Hey,

I wanted to share a little story today — something that might resonate if you've ever felt those nervous butterflies about starting something new.

This week, I found myself in a bit of a pickle. I needed to start my swimming training at a new pool. Now, I know what you're thinking: "It's just a pool, what's the big deal?" But oh, the stories my mind started spinning! Would the lanes be crowded? Would I find a good spot? Is my back recovered enough? Is there a safe space for my stuff?

I had all these little worries, and let me tell you, they were loud. I could've come up with a million excuses to put off going — it was Wednesday and it was windy! I could've stayed comfy and cosy in my old routine. But here's the thing: comfort zones are sneaky. They feel safe, but they also hold us back from discovering something amazing.

So, I took a deep breath and dove in (pun intended). And guess what? It was all perfectly fine. No freezing water, no lane hogs, just a nice, refreshing return to my cardio swim. All those nerves and stories I'd built up in my head were just that — stories. The unknown wasn't so scary after all. (Even if I did go the wrong way and get lost before I'd even found the entrance door.)

I know how daunting it can be to step out of your comfort zone, whether it's trying a new activity, meeting new people, or just showing up somewhere different. But here's a little trick I've learned: focus on one small thing you can control. For me, it was simply showing up at the pool. Once I was there, everything else fell into place.

Next time you're facing something new, try to embrace the adventure. Let yourself be curious instead of cautious. And remember, the unknown often turns out to be just fine — sometimes even better than fine!

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
