Hey,

Well, I admit, I stuffed up. It's currently 7:31am on a Sunday morning and I've just sat down to write this. I usually have time blocked out on a Thursday to craft this little gem, but this week got away from me — a few extra sessions popped up, covering unexpected classes, and organising the Glow Flow event last night meant lights and a speaker pack to pick up.

We can't be 100%, 100% of the time. (Read that again.)

Life's juggling balls are going to drop sometimes. Whether it's in your training, nutrition, mindset, workload, or the kids' schedules. Sometimes life can run like a well-oiled machine. Other times it can feel like being stuck on a runaway train!

Embrace that you are only human. Here are three things that can help you navigate when life feels like that speeding train:

**Prioritise.** Check out the four quadrants — visually put into four sections: important, not important, urgent, not urgent. Choose what the priorities are at that moment.

**Self-care.** I know, I know — I hear you saying, "But Nikki, there's so much to do, how could I possibly take time for myself?" Self-care can be as quick or elaborate as you like. It can be as simple as pausing for five minutes to breathe — focusing on yourself at that moment and calming the overactive nervous system. Taking the time to stop and gather thoughts for a few moments can help you make the decisions you need to make above.

**Delegate.** It's okay to ask for help. Or, depending on your social circle, you may even have people offering. Again, we're only human; we can't do everything. Everyone has strengths and weaknesses — your source of overwhelm may be where someone else thrives and is happy to help.

I take full ownership: I let a ball drop this week. But I did have an amazing time running the Glow Flow event last night, and welcoming new humans into the Jungle Botany tribe as well as the Coach Nikki Ninja crew.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
