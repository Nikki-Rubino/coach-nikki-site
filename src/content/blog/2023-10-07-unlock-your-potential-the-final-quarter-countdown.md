Hey,

It's hard to believe there are only three months left in 2023! We're in the final quarter.

It's the time of year I see many clients able to do things that were a struggle a few months ago. Health and wellness is not just about the physical. Take a moment to reflect on what you've done, achieved, conquered, or simply introduced to add positivity or balance to your own health and wellness. Celebrate the small wins so far, no matter how small and insignificant they seem right now.

Each of us has unique objectives and dreams, and now is the perfect moment to reaffirm our commitment to them. Whether it's personal growth, career development, health and wellbeing, or any other goal you've set, remember that you have the potential to make incredible strides in the next three months.

The year may or may not have panned out how you envisioned. But just because you get one flat tyre doesn't mean you'd go and slash the other three, right? Don't think about the obstacles. Think about what next year will look like if you don't start these habits now.

Here are a few thoughts to help guide your journey as we approach the year's end:

**Set clear goals.** Revisit your goals for the year and break them down into manageable, actionable steps. Clarity is the first step towards achievement.

**Stay consistent.** Consistency is the key to progress. Create a routine that supports your goals and stick to it, even when faced with challenges or distractions. Accountability is key — tell someone, or write it down.

**Stay resilient.** The path to success is rarely without obstacles. Embrace setbacks as learning opportunities and use them to fuel your determination.

**Seek support.** Don't hesitate to reach out to mentors, friends or colleagues for guidance. Together, we can achieve more than we can alone.

**Celebrate small wins.** Acknowledge and celebrate your achievements along the way. Small victories add up and provide the motivation needed to keep going.

**Adapt and evolve.** Be open to adjusting your strategies if necessary. Flexibility is a valuable trait in the pursuit of success.

It's never too late! The final months of the year offer a fresh opportunity to recommit to your goals and make significant progress. Whatever you want — go and get it. And if you need some help, reach out.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
