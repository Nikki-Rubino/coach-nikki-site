**Quick movement wins for the season**

Staying active during the holidays doesn't need to be a marathon effort (unless you love marathons — then go for it!). Movement can help combat those stressful times, getting you out of your head and back into your body. Try these:

- **Kitchen counter mobility:** waiting for the kettle to boil? Pop into a deep squat, do some elevated front steps, or simply practise wrist rolls to prep for unwrapping gifts.
- **Living room flow:** while the Christmas movie credits roll, drop into a Travelling Beast or Scorpion Reach for 2 minutes. Animal Flow = festive magic in motion, no matter what level you are.
- **Staircase hustle:** if you've got stairs, use them! A few rounds of step-ups or calf raises keep your energy high — or simply time how many laps you can do.
- **Dance magic:** whether it's in the kitchen, lounge room, or out in the fresh air — move those feet, shake those hips, and wave your arms like you just don't care. You may be surprised how the body reacts.

**Small steps, big impact.** The best part? It doesn't have to be perfect to be powerful. A quick 5 minutes of movement can help shake off holiday stress, boost your mood, and keep you feeling strong — ready for whatever December throws your way.
