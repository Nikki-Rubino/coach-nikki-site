Hey,

This week I'm prepping to head off to Bali for an exciting, week-long Animal Flow mentorship! Programs are written, coach covers are sorted, and I've prepped all the admin for while I'm gone. This way I can fully immerse myself in connections with other amazing humans, and purely succumb to being a student — and a fellow mentor to the younger, less-experienced flowists in the mix. I'm sure you've heard me talk about how much I love being a student, and this is the perfect chance to sharpen my skills. I'll be learning from some of the best in the business from across the globe, deepening my knowledge, and bringing back so much to share with you all, no doubt!

This is the third Animal Flow mentorship under my belt. As much as I love this movement practice, I also love connecting with the people in the global community.

One of the things I love most about being a lifelong learner is how it keeps us humble, curious, and adaptable. It's not just about mastering new movements but also about keeping our bodies and minds sharp. Whether it's Animal Flow, strength training, or even just learning to move better, growth keeps us energised and vibrant.

So, while I'm away, think about how you can continue to learn and grow. What's one thing you'd like to upskill? And if you're not currently training with me, still get involved — if not from a movement perspective, then a hobby, a passion project, or a subject you'd like to upskill in.

Stay tuned — I'll be sharing more from Bali soon.

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
