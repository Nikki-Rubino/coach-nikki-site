Hey,

Is it just me, or did we *blink* and suddenly it's mid-November? (Side question… is it acceptable to put the Christmas tree up now? Asking for a friend.)

This time of year can feel like a mental marathon — finishing up work projects, juggling social events, sorting presents, and navigating family dynamics. Add in the kids' "I want, I need" requests and things can spiral fast. Even I'm not immune; my brain's already off planning next year's events, programming ideas, and studio steps.

So here's my reminder, for you and for me — take a **breath reset**. Ten minutes. That's all.

Find a spot that feels good, set a timer, and either sit in silence or pop on some music or a guided meditation. Try this simple rhythm:

- Breathe in for 4 counts
- Breathe out for 4–6 counts

Or, if you want to spice it up, try the **box breath**: inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Repeat.

I've given myself an 8-day mission to sit still each day, listen to *East Forest*, and journal whatever comes up. If you need a little grounding, I invite you to join me.

Our bodies remember more than we realise. Today would have been my big sister's 46th birthday. It's been 15 years since I gained a guardian angel, yet every year I still feel it — the heaviness, the fog, the ache that sits quietly under the surface. So I move through it. Each year I swim the same number of laps as her age would have been — no headphones, no distractions, just me, the water, and that slow push through resistance until I find peace on the other side.

Whatever this time of year brings for you — joy, stress, nostalgia, or all of it mixed together — I hope you give yourself permission to pause. Just 10 minutes to breathe, reset, and remember that you're doing great, even if it doesn't always feel like it.

> "The mind and body are not separate. What affects one, affects the other."

Stay Sassy and big love to you,
Coach Nikki
