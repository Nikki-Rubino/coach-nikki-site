Hey,

This week, I couldn't help but share something close to my heart: my parents just celebrated 50 years of marriage and friendship with two other couples who tied the knot on the same day! They met on their honeymoon in Hayman Island, and five decades later, they're still making memories together. (Yes, they're that iconic.)

What's their secret? A shared love of adventure, humour, and staying active — plus the ability to laugh at themselves while "ageing disgracefully" (their words, not mine!).

It got me thinking about how fitness and friendship go hand in hand. Just like those long-lasting bonds my parents have nurtured, building strength, mobility, and resilience isn't something you do alone. Surrounding yourself with a supportive community makes the journey way more fun and rewarding — whether it's friends cheering you on in a workout or swapping stories post-class over coffee.

Why fitness + friendship is a winning combo: accountability (friends won't let you skip your training); laughter = stress relief (a workout buddy who makes you laugh can turn a tough session into a highlight of your day); and shared milestones (achieving a goal together makes it all the more meaningful).

So, what can you do this week to combine fitness and friendship? Maybe it's inviting a friend to join you on a walk, trying something new like Animal Flow (hint: more classes coming in 2025), or celebrating a small movement win together. Remember, fitness isn't just about the reps — it's about building a life full of moments you'll cherish, just like my parents and their lifelong friends.

Let's make some memories (and maybe some muscles) together. Ready?

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
