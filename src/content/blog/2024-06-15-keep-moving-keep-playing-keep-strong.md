Hey,

Who said we have to stop having fun just because we're getting older? Certainly not me!

Two weeks ago I reached level 41 in life. What I'm most inspired about is the number of retirees I work with at the moment gallivanting to the other side of the world. Now that is something to keep looking forward to — seeing the rest of the world I haven't reached yet.

Did you know the secret to staying young at heart and vibrant in body as we age? It's all about keeping our strength, movement, and playfulness alive!

**Strength:** remember when lifting that suitcase felt like hoisting a feather? Let's keep it that way! Regular strength training keeps our muscles sturdy and our bones happy. Plus, it gives us a good excuse to inspire others around us with what we can do.

**Movement:** ever noticed how kids never sit still? They've got it right! Keeping active — whether it's a brisk walk, a dance-off in the living room, or an Animal Flow session — helps us stay nimble and energised. Let's move like nobody's watching and keep our bodies in top gear!

**Play:** here's the secret sauce — play! Yes, play isn't just for kids. It's for the young at heart too. Games, hobbies and spontaneous adventures keep our minds sharp and our spirits high. So why not challenge your friends to a game of tennis (or maybe something less intense, like Scrabble)?

In a nutshell, staying strong, moving freely, and keeping the playfulness alive is our recipe for a joyous, healthy life. Let's embrace every moment and show the world that ageing is just another word for living fabulously.

Catch you on the dance floor?

P.S. If you need a dance partner, my light-up shoes are always charged and ready.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
