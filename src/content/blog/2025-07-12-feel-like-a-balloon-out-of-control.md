Hey,

Ever have those moments where there are so many ideas and not enough hours, and your brain feels like it's tab-hopping like a laptop on 1% battery? Yep. Me too.

Right now I feel like a balloon that's been let go — no direction, just a slow, dramatic spiral into the corner of the room. I'm busy… but not productive. And if you know me, you'll know I take a lot of pride in showing up with useful, quality info — no fluff, just the good stuff. But lately, that standard has started to eat into my own battery life.

Some weeks the most honest thing we can do is notice that we're busy but not *productive*, and gently reset rather than push harder. It's not throwing in the towel — it's grabbing a better one that's less frayed around the edges.

So a couple of reflection prompts for you, the same ones I've been sitting with: **why do you keep coming back to the things that fuel you — and what do you actually want more of?** More stories, more tips, more real-life wins, more movement nuggets, more sass? Knowing the answer makes it so much easier to spend your limited energy on what matters.

Thanks for being here.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
