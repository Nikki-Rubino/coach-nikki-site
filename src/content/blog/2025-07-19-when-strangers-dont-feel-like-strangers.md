Hey,

If a friend asked me to walk into a room, lock eyes with a stranger, and start a conversation… that's a **big fat no** from me. BUT, if that stranger happens to share a passion I love, has been vouched for by someone I adore, and we already have something weird and wonderful in common? Well then, *that's a hell yes*. And before you know it, we're rolling and playing around the floor together (literally — welcome to the world of Animal Flow).

**This is the magic of community.** It's not just training. It's not just sweat and skill and sore wrists from Levitating Side Kick Thrus. It's connection. It's walking into a room, or a park, or a new country, and feeling like you already *belong*.

Just last week, a Flowist from Brooklyn was visiting Sydney. A mutual friend from the same Brooklyn crew said, "You two HAVE to meet." And because of that one connection, we trained, laughed, and swapped stories like old mates. Just like that, another thread was woven into the web of this global community.

When was the last time you felt part of something like that? Not the 'nod at your gym neighbour while avoiding awkward conversation' kind, but the real-deal, energising, fun-as-hell kind of community? If it's been a while, come flow with us. Try a class. Say yes to something new. You never know who you'll meet, or how much they might inspire you.

What is something new you can try this week?

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
