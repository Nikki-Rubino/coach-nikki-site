Hey,

We know the importance of sleep — we need it for our brains and bodies to literally function, and function well. So how do we implement a sleep routine?

Well, I ask you: what relaxes you? Start with that.

Aim to hit at least 20 to 30 minutes of downtime before settling into bed, with whatever is relaxing for you — read, take a bath, paint, meditate, ideally anything away from a screen. Then, just before bed, start to give your body signs that it's time to sleep. What do I mean by this? Give yourself subtle little hints, from smell or touch, that it's time to sleep now. An example could be:

- 20 minutes of relaxing downtime
- Take a bath or shower
- Put on scented lotion or spray a calming mist
- Put on PJs — the new texture on your skin is a tactile cue that something has changed
- Hop into bed and do something calming to drown out the day's thoughts: read, breathwork, gratitude journal

Whatever the sequence is for you, make it a habit and routine. Give your body a chance to adapt and recognise the cues — at least 21 days. Make it as simple as you like, and make it resonate with you.

Mine, for example, lasts about 15 to 30 minutes: relax time is my paint-by-numbers and chill mixes; a shower or simply washing my face and putting on face moisturiser; three minutes of hip and shoulder release; then my head on a silk pillowcase (textures help me relax).

Create a few subtle wind-down cues for yourself, and give your body time to pick up on them. I'd love to know what routines you come up with that work for you.
