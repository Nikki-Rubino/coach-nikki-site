Hey,

Yes, I'm aware this little love note is arriving fashionably late. My body decided to hit the *mandatory rest* button — battery flashing red — and it's shaping up to be another sprint to the end of the month.

Now, I've noticed a *theme* popping up lately. A lot of us have been skipping movement thanks to work chaos, soggy skies, and energy levels best described as "sloth on a Sunday."

So here's the plan: for the next five weeks I'm recycling some golden oldies and giving you **one movement a day** to try. Simple, doable, no two-hour gym commitments. Just quick, tasty *movement snacks* to sprinkle into your day.

Do one daily. Do them all at once. Do them in your pyjamas. I don't care — just move. Your future self will thank you, and your current self might just get a cheeky energy boost.

Now go snack on some movement, and let me know which one resonates with you this week.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
