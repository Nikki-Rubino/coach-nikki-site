Hey,

Keeping it short and sweet today, and totally relevant. A huge thank you to those who have put up with my lack of brain capacity the last few weeks — more on that story next week.

Ever had one of those days where your brain and body just aren't on speaking terms? One of my clients walked in last week feeling off — a heavy mental load, broken sleep, and a body that just wasn't cooperating.

The usual strength and flow? Not happening. Instead of forcing it, we switched things up. Less pressure, more movement that felt right in the moment. Fast forward a week — mental stress lifted, sleep improved, and BAM… she hit a PB!

Training (like life) isn't linear. Sleep, nutrition, stress, mental load, inflammation — it all plays a role. Some days, you push. Some days, you pivot. Regression isn't failure — it's strategy.

So next time you feel off, ask yourself: what does my body need today? Listen to it, adjust, and trust the process.

Keep moving and stay Sassy,
Nikki
