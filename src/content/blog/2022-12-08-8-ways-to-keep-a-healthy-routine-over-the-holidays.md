Hi Friend!

Who else is freaking out that December has snuck up so quickly once again? It can be a different emotional time of year for everyone.

Either way, there's a final push to the end of the year — work deadlines, planning a getaway, finishing loose ends, strategising for the year to come, or social activities in abundance. Life ramps up before it slows down. Today I share some tips to keep a healthy routine for the mind and body as the end-of-year chaos approaches.

**1. Be kind to yourself.** You cannot serve others if you are not serving yourself. Drink that water, be sure to move, and take time to refill your own teapot with things you enjoy.

**2. Move how you can.** A 30-minute movement session is still valid. You may not have time for your full routine, and that's ok. Any movement is better than none. Focus on compound lifts like deadlifts and squats to hit all areas.

**3. You don't have to do it all.** You could be invited to so many social activities. Reality check: choose the ones that resonate, the ones you'll enjoy. Don't feel obliged to go to all of them.

**4. Make a plan.** With a solid foundation set, you can go into things with the best intention. Look at your week ahead and schedule the non-negotiables of movement, self-care, and nourishment. When will you train? When will you recharge? What's your fuel? Then enjoy the company you're with.

**5. Set boundaries — with yourself.** Limit the weekly units of alcohol, up the servings of fresh foods, get your minimal daily walks in. Do your own admin and keep accountable.

**6. Have a back-up plan.** The day might not go to plan. If a gym session gets bumped by work, don't let it derail everything. An extra walk or an at-home session is all you need. Trust me — you'll feel a whole lot better with *any* movement, as opposed to "oh, it's screwed up, may as well sit on the couch."

**7. Social is sexy.** Movement doesn't have to take away from the catch-ups. Schedule an activity with a friend instead of a long lunch — or both. A morning walk, a game of putt-putt, bowling, a moving coffee catch-up instead of a sit-down one.

**8. It's ok to rest.** With all the social obligations, it can be very overwhelming and draining to be around that many people. Recharge however it suits you.

Be kind — you may not know what others are going through this time of year.
