Hey,

Well, winter has indeed struck. It's getting colder and the wind has a fresh bite to it.

Now tell me — have you recently experienced chapped lips, dry skin, feeling tired and irritable, or a loss of appetite? You might simply put it down to the change of weather. But my next question is: have you neglected your water intake? I know I'm guilty of this as the weather gets cooler.

Our bodies are made up of 50 to 70% water. They cannot function to their full capacity if dehydrated. We need water for digestion, to absorb nutrients, to help us move, to get rid of waste products, and to regulate our body temperature.

From a movement perspective, we need water to keep our bodies manoeuvrable. Muscle tissue, tendons and fascia need moisture to have their full range potential. Think of your insides like a sponge: moisture equals pliable, dehydration equals fragile.

So here's my public service announcement to continue to drink water, ideally 30ml per kilogram of bodyweight. Let me know your favourite way to get your liquids in through the winter.

Until next week,
Nikki
