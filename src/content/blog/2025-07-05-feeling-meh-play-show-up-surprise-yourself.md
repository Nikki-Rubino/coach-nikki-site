Hey,

Let's talk about *those* weeks. You know the ones — where motivation packs its bags and disappears. When the wind outside sounds like it's trying to evict your roof, your blanket is holding you hostage, and Netflix just started another season you didn't ask for. Yep. That was me this week. Physically I could move, but mentally I was a puddle of "ugh."

But here's what saved me: **a coach.** One who knows how to push my buttons in the best way.

It was Wednesday morning, Sydney being attacked by the wind gods, and I had my own 9am session. Did I want to go? Nope. Did I go? *Sigh* — yes. Am I glad I did? Hell yes. Pauly (my coach) knew I wasn't vibing, so instead of barking reps at me, he got cheeky. We played: freestyle flow, tuck-ups, and shuffles. He knew what I loved and used it to shift my brain back *into my body*. Then, sneaky bugger that he is, we added load, reps, and intent.

Across three sessions this week — two of them flying solo where I felt like a sloth in a human costume — I did the only thing I could muster: **I showed up.** Not with fireworks. Just showed up. And somehow heavy things were lifted, technique got refined, there was chat, there was play, and there was proof that even on the "off" weeks, you can still get it done.

So if this week feels a little *meh*, here's what I suggest:

- **Show up** — not to crush it, but to check in.
- **Find some play** — joy is fuel.
- **Adjust the dial** — not every session needs to be a 10/10.
- **Have a plan** (or a coach) who knows how to read the room… aka you.

You won't always feel motivated — especially as women, our energy naturally shifts throughout the month. That's not weakness, that's *intelligence* in motion. You're allowed to train accordingly: to modify, to simmer instead of boil, to show up and surprise yourself.

So if this week needs to be a simmer, not a sizzle — I see you. And I'll be here next week when you're ready to crank it back up.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
