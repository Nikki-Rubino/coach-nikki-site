Hey,

How are you holding up? If your brain feels like it's running on 1% battery right now, you're not alone. This time of year can feel like an endurance event, but instead of medals, we're chasing deadlines, shopping lists, and festive obligations. 🎁

Whether you're planning holiday gatherings (did someone say nut-free, gluten-free, dairy-free pavlova?), juggling work wrap-ups like a circus performer, navigating overcrowded shops (seriously, where did all these people come from?!), opening a new semi-private PT studio in Matraville (I don't remember this being on my original 2024 Santa wishlist 😆), or just trying to remember who's bringing what to Christmas lunch… it's no wonder your brain is waving the white flag. 🧠

But here's the thing: you don't have to pour from an empty cup.

**3 ways to outsmart the brain drain**

1. Move before you lose (your cool). Physical activity isn't just good for your body; it's a reset button for your brain. Go for a walk, do some stretches, or even flow through a few Animal Flow moves. Bonus: movement helps you pause before you send that cranky email or snap in the checkout line. 😅
2. Pick one thing & crush it. Multitasking = brain overload. Choose one task, set a timer for 15 minutes, and go for it. The satisfaction of ticking it off your list will recharge your motivation.
3. Say "no" with a smile. Not every invite, task, or last-minute Secret Santa needs your attention. Protect your energy by setting boundaries. Saying "no" (nicely!) is saying "yes" to a calmer, happier you.

You can't do it all, and you don't have to. Take a deep breath (or ten). Prioritise what truly matters, and give yourself permission to rest when you need it. You've got this, and I'm cheering for you every step of the way!

Here's to moving through the chaos with strength, flow, a little bit of fun and a sprinkle of sass.

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
