Hi Friend,

So here we are, getting to the nitty-gritty end of the year. You may be prepping to spend time with family, friends, or simply by yourself.

You are allowed to rest and recharge. This is the time of year when you could be winding down and getting an adventure of some sort. That adventure can be welcomed, or it can bring on bouts of anxious thoughts about the year ahead. Here are some useful tips to remain relatively calm, or at least have a sense of control over the transition.

**1. Write it down.** To-do lists, checklists, audio lists — however your brain deals with information, brain-dump all the tasks taking up space in your mind. Then prioritise and make a plan of attack. You'll feel a lot better having it laid out in front of you. Note what to get done *before* you take a break, and *after* you return.

**2. Log out.** Tell people about your away dates. Even if it's a staycation and you're at home catching up on Netflix or a book, it's YOUR recharge time. Log out or silence emails and other work software.

**3. Gamify the day.** Bust out the old-school board games, the Wii Sing or Dance, the cards. Get some good old human interaction, laughter, and banter into the day.

**4. Watch or read an old favourite.** A new or old movie with someone of choice (or just your dog and a gin). Unwind, relax, maybe have a laugh or a jump-scare thrill. Ninety to 120 minutes of entering another world. Or maybe the couch, a blanket, and a good book is more your vibe.

**5. Get outside.** Fresh air and connecting with nature is such a great way to revitalise. Go for a simple walk or plan something fun with a friend. Move however it makes your heart dance.

**6. You are special too.** Remember to treat yourself. We can get caught up servicing others over Christmas — no one is as important as you. Treat yourself with whatever sparks a little joy.

Recharge however your lil heart desires. You deserve it.

Until next week, Stay Sassy,
Nikki
