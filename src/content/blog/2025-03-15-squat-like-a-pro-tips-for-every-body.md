So last week I shared some visual references to regressed squat positions. Now let me preface: every physical body is different, and that is totally OK!

Things to take into consideration when squatting:

- Ankle mobility
- Femur and torso lengths
- Hip mobility
- Spine mobility

Your foot position may be different to the person next to you. You ideally want to be slightly wider than hips, facing forward or turned out up to 5 degrees, and you want even weight within your foot tripod — big toe, little toe and heel.

With all of that being said, and individual to each person, let's discuss the mechanics of a generic loaded squat. Now, there are many, many MANY variations of squats — today I'm keeping it basic.
