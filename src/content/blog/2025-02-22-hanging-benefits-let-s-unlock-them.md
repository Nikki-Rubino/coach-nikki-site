Hey,

Hope your shoulders are feeling great after last week's activation drills! Now that we're unlocking new movement, let's talk about hanging. Yep, good old-fashioned hanging — it's not just for monkeys or kids in the playground!

Hanging is one of the best things you can do for your spine (hello, decompression!) and your grip strength (because who doesn't want strong, capable hands?). Whether you're opening stubborn jars, holding onto the shopping bags in one trip (like a champion), or just wanting to feel more connected to your upper body, grip strength matters.

But Nikki, I hear you ask — how do I start? No worries, I've got you sorted! Even if you can't do a full hang yet, you can modify it and still reap the benefits. Try it out, and for extra fun, mix it with last week's activation drills. Your shoulders, spine, and hands will love you for it! Don't have a bar? No problem — I personally have a suction-cup chin-up bar attached in my ensuite.

Give it a go and let me know how it feels!

Keep moving and stay Sassy,
Nikki
