Hey,

Did you know that your ankles play a huge role in keeping you active and injury-free as you age?

Think about your ankle angle every time you need to take a step down. Are you someone who needs to turn sideways to gain stability walking down a single step?

**Why ankle mobility matters**

- **Balance and stability:** strong, flexible ankles help prevent falls and maintain balance.
- **Efficient movement:** good ankle mobility means smoother walking, running, and even dancing!
- **Pain prevention:** stiff ankles can lead to knee, hip and back pain. Let's avoid that, shall we?

Fun fact: your ankles support your entire body weight with every step. That's some serious responsibility!

**Easy exercises to improve ankle mobility**

- **Calf release:** equipment needed — a foam roller, PVC pipe or anything cylindrical that can hold the weight of one leg.
- **Calf stretch:** stand facing a wall, step one foot back, keep both heels down, and gently bend the front knee. Hold for 30 seconds, then switch.
- **Shin raises:** stand with feet flat, then lift your toes while keeping heels on the ground. Repeat 15 times.

Make these exercises part of your daily routine and your ankles will thank you!

Bonus challenge: try balancing on one foot while brushing your teeth. It's a fun way to sneak in some ankle work!

Let's keep those ankles agile and your adventures limitless.
