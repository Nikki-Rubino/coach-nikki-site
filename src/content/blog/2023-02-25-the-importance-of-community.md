Hey,

"Who you spend time with is who you become." — Tony Robbins

Last week I visited a big-box gym. I used to be a member of these chains a long time ago — I'd get up early, do my one-hour workout, and sit at my TV editing job the long rest of the day. It's all I knew about health and fitness! Either jumping into all the high-volume, high-intensity classes, or putting on my headphones and doing my own thing.

Seven years into coaching, I'm more aware of the holistic side of health and fitness, and that there's so much more to it than simply getting my pump on and getting out. Don't get me wrong — if the gym is your quiet space for alone time and it works for you, great. But what struck me as soon as I entered this space was a sterile, corporate feeling. No one talking to each other, no human interaction, no smiles, laughs or emotions whatsoever. It got me thinking, and hoping that these people have a community outside of there.

So what is community? In simple terms, it's collectively a group of people with a common interest. For a community to be strong, it must enrich the lives of each individual and give a sense of trust, connection and caring for each other.

The benefits of having access to a supportive community:

**Social connection and engagement.** As humans, we need positive interactions with others; without them it can have profound effects on our health. A safe place to have conversations, share stories and insights. These interactions can result in better health, from reduced stress, to improved immune function, to a lower risk of dementia as we age. Conversation keeps our minds active!

**A sense of belonging and purpose.** Collective solutions help individual problems. Alone, we can feel lost, overwhelmed and frazzled. Having access to supportive people can help reduce stress and anxiety, improve mental health, and give you a place to thrive.

Community is something that is new to my life, and I'm forever grateful to the two supportive networks of Jungle Brothers and Animal Flow. May you too have access to somewhere that makes you smile, and feel valued and loved!

Until next week,
Nikki
