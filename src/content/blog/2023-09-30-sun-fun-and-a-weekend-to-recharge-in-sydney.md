Hey,

It's a long weekend here in Sydney!

The clocks will change, the weather is warm and sunny, and it's more than likely an out-of-routine weekend.

It's okay to let your hair down, have a sleep-in, get out in nature, spend time with family and friends, and simply do things that are your recharge. Nutrition may be a little off course for a few days — that's okay! We don't have to be 100%, 100% of the time.

Enjoy some time off, or a skeleton staff if you're still working — some time to think, recharge and create memories. Whether it's a full weekend or simply a solo recharge, you do you. Do whatever makes your heart dance, with the people who bring you joy.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
