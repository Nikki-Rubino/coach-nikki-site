Hey,

If you've ever wondered whether a squat and a deadlift are the same thing (or which one you should be doing), you're not alone!

They're both powerhouse moves for strength and mobility, but they work differently. Both are a staple of the Coach Nikki Ninjas, and across this month I'll be breaking down the differences. Here's the simplest way to break it down:

**🦵 Squat = knee-dominant, a vertical movement**

Think of sitting down and standing up — or, as I say, the elevator. Hips and knees bend together, keeping the chest more upright and eyes forward (not to the floor). Heels down, bum low, proud chest, eyes forward. What muscles are you working? Quads, glutes, and core stability, with upper back as an accessory depending on what you're holding.

**⚡ Deadlift = hip-dominant, horizontal torso**

Think of picking up a laundry basket, or closing a door with your bum because your hands are full. More hip hinge (sending the bum and hips backwards), with the knees bending less. Or, if you train with me, you more than likely understand the "shine your bum torch to the wall" cue. Fires up glutes, hamstrings, calves and all of your back.

Both are essential, no matter what level in life you are. Squats help with getting up from chairs, while deadlifts keep our backs strong for lifting and carrying.
