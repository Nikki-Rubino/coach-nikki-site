Hey Friend,

Last Sunday for Father's Day we watched *The Nonnas* — and wow, did those ladies dish out some wisdom (along with pasta, of course). One line really stuck with me:

> "When you stop learning, playing, and growing… that's when you really get old."

Boom. Straight to the heart. And honestly? Spot on — it's how I used to sign off these emails. Because here's the truth:

* **Learning** keeps your brain switched on (and stops you swearing at the remote).
* **Playing** keeps life fun — laughter, silliness, dancing in the kitchen, all count.
* **Growing** means you don't just *get through* the week… you actually thrive.

Which is exactly why I run the **Strong and Sassy Squad** — for legends 60+ who are ready to *play, get strong, and keep growing*, twice a week, in good company. We move, laugh, learn, and throw in a little sass of our own (you know I can't help myself).

So if that sounds like your kind of fun — or you know someone who needs this in their life — come and find me. Because let's face it: you're not "too old." You're just getting started.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
