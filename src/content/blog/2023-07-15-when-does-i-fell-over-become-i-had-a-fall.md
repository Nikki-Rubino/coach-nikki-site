Hey,

Have you ever realised there's a point in life where we change "I fell over" to "I had a fall"?

Why is it that when we're younger, it can feel like there are so many people invested in you — wanting to see you thrive: parents, friends, bosses, co-workers. Then at some point, as you get older, the emphasis gets shifted to the younger crew.

My mission is to make Sassy Seniors feel seen and heard again. To encourage the generation that encouraged me!

To quote Meryl Streep: "People don't love you for how you look. What matters is what you bring into the world, the smiles you can bring to people's faces."

Everyone deserves the right to freedom of movement and joy in day-to-day life. Getting active is not simply about adding years to your life; it's about adding life to your years. We can improve our brain function with activities like crosswords and Sudoku, but these can't compare with the benefits of exercise on the brain. Movement aids cognitive function, balance, coordination and problem-solving, and so much more, all while gaining confidence.

Do you know someone who has dimmed their smile as they got older, and would benefit from gaining movement independence back?

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
