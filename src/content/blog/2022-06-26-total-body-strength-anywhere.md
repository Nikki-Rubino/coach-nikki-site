Hey,

How did the Crab Reach feel last week? Did you manage to incorporate it into your day after sitting?

This week I'm sharing one move for core, shoulders and hips — the side kick through — and how to put three of these movements together to build a "flow" sequence. A short movement routine you can take with you anywhere.

Benefits of the side kick through:

- Upper body rotation strength
- Hip flexor strength
- Hand-eye coordination
- Chest strength and mobility
- Upper back activation

Now let's put it all together from the last three weeks. This short Animal Flow sequence is a must that I give to all my desk workers, beginner flowists, those needing upper body awareness, and those with lower back niggles:

- Crab Reach
- Underswitch
- Side Kick Through

Drill each move for about 60 seconds, do two to three rounds, then piece it all together. Let me know how you go.

Until next week — new month, new topic!
