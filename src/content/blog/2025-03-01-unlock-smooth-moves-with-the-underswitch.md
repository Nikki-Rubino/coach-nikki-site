Hey,

We've stretched, we've activated, we've hung like sassy apes — and now, it's time to move. Enter: the underswitch.

This little gem from the Animal Flow system is a game-changer for shoulder strength, mobility, coordination, and just feeling downright smooth in your movement, no matter what level in life you are. Plus, it's a brilliant way to start linking your newfound range and strength into actual movement, with the added benefit of brain power and a hell of a lot of whole-core control.

This — and regressed variations — is a staple in all of my programming. Some of you are doing the full variation, others are sliding knees across the floor, others are working on a box. There are some great tutorials out there, including from Mike Fitch, the creator of Animal Flow.

This move might feel a bit funky at first, but stick with it! You'll be weaving in and out of the floor like a ninja in no time. And bonus — it ties in perfectly with everything we've worked on so far. Strong shoulders? ✅ Hand strength? ✅ Stability and coordination? ✅

Give it a crack and let me know how you go!

Keep moving and stay Sassy,
Nikki
