Hey,

It's Christmas Eve, and I hope this email finds you wrapped in the warmth of the holiday spirit! Whether you're a festive enthusiast or navigating a more reflective time, remember that everyone has a unique story during this season.

**The tapestry of Christmas memories.** Christmas, like a cosy blanket, can evoke a spectrum of emotions. For some it's a joyous celebration filled with laughter; for others it may carry the weight of bittersweet memories or the absence of loved ones. Let's tread lightly and remember Harry Styles' timeless advice: "treat people with kindness".

**Lost in time… and Tetris!** Ah, the week between Christmas and New Year's — a time when calendars seem more like Tetris games. Days lose their names, and we navigate through a sea of leftovers and planned activities. Let's embrace the chaos with a dash of humour and a generous helping of kindness.

**Reflect, relax, and revel in rest.** Amidst the festivities, don't forget that this is also a time for self-care. Whether it's reflecting on the year gone by, relaxing with a good book, or catching up on much-needed rest, let yourself indulge in the activities that bring you peace.

**The 1.91% extravaganza!** Feeling guilty about that extra chocolate or extended leftovers feast? Fear not! In the grand scheme of things, this festive week is just a tiny slice — a mere 1.91% — of the entire year. So savour the moments, create memories, and enjoy the company of those you cherish.

**Indulge and bounce back.** Yes, chocolate, gin and leftovers will be on my agenda too! Let's revel in the joy of food, movement and the company of loved ones. And when the week of indulgence concludes, have a plan to hop back on the routine train. It's all about balance and enjoying the ride.

Wishing you a festive season filled with laughter, joy and a sprinkle of magic. Thank you for being a part of this community and for sharing the weekly journey. May your days be merry and bright! I'm officially off the working clock until January 8th.

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
