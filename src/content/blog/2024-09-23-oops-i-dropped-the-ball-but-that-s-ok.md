Hey,

You know how life can be a bit like a roller coaster, right? 🎢 Well, this past week felt just like that!

So, I missed a few things — Sunday's usual 7am email, some program updates, and my food prep was basically non-existent. Why? Well, last weekend was the annual Glow Flow event. With tickets to sell, rooms to transform, and plans to adjust for different skill levels, it was all systems go.

Then, 30 minutes before the event, I got news of a family death. They'd been unwell, but it was a bit unexpected. With the event starting soon and nothing I could do in that moment, I had to put on my "service face" — a skill honed in hospitality. Despite everything, the event was a hit, and only those close to me knew something was off.

The next day, I headed to the Central Coast to be with family. Shout out to my fellow JB coach who helped with the logistics, allowing me to stay as long as needed. Sure, the admin didn't get done, but it was a reminder that I have the flexibility now to step away when needed — something I couldn't always do in my advertising days.

By week's end, I was assisting at an Animal Flow certification workshop on the north side. I had to let go of the guilt of what didn't get done and focus on the support and community around me.

It's a lesson I keep learning: life doesn't have to be perfect, and that's perfectly okay. Community and conversations really are everything — don't forget to lean on yours!

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
