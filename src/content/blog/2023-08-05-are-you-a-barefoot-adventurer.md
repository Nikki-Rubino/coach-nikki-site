Hey,

Are you a fellow barefoot adventurer? This week I met, assessed and added three Sassy Seniors into the weekly mix. One common thing was noticeable in affecting balance — can you guess it? Their foot control and weight distribution during walking and sit-to-stand. Your feet are literally your base of support, but are you actually challenging the 29 muscles associated with each one?

Let's explore three delightful benefits of walking barefoot:

**Ninja-like foot strength.** Ditching those restrictive shoes and letting your tootsies roam free will transform your feet into powerful little ninjas. Those sneaky small muscles in your feet and toes will get a workout like never before. You'll be tip-toeing through life with grace and agility, just like a true ninja master!

**Sensory delight extravaganza.** Say hello to a sensory party down at your feet! I talk a lot about the sensory system in our hands and feet with Animal Flow practice. Walking barefoot is like a thrilling ride for your senses — a symphony of textures, from ticklish grass to grainy sand, and even the occasional squishy mud puddle (don't worry, it's all part of the fun). Prepare to be amazed as you tune in to the world beneath you like never before.

**Stress-busting earth hugs.** Hug it out with Mother Earth! You may have heard of grounding. When your feet make direct contact with the ground, it's like a magical earth-hug that soothes your soul and melts away stress. Talk about a natural chill pill! So next time life gets tough, remember: all you need is a barefoot walk to get back on the happy trail. Or simply start the day with a barefoot walk around the backyard.

Barefoot walking is for anyone who craves a taste of freedom, strength and posture benefits with a sprinkle of adventure in their daily life. So unleash your inner wild child, and let your feet embark on a wondrous barefoot journey. Happy barefoot wandering!

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
