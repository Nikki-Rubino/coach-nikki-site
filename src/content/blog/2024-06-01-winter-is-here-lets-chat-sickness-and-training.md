Hey,

Winter is here!

And with it comes a wave of sickness. In just the last two weeks, I've seen pneumonia, flu, COVID and simple colds affecting friends, clients, and even some of our fellow JB coaches. The weather has turned chilly, and with our immune systems still rebuilding from all that indoor time, it's no surprise that germs are making the rounds.

I remember my advertising days when I would get sick at the drop of a hat, especially during winter. My asthmatic lungs would flare up, and I'd find myself congested at least once a month. But over the years, I've found some strategies that have significantly boosted my immune system.

Today, I want to share my top four tips to help you stay healthy this winter.

1. **Quality sleep.** Rest is crucial. Sleep is our body's natural recovery system. Without optimal sleep, we crave non-nutritional foods, struggle to focus, and are more prone to injury.

2. **Quality foods.** Nutrition is key! My go-to tip is swapping a daily hot beverage for bone broth. Eat for fuel, focusing on anti-inflammatory foods. If injuries are disrupting your sleep (like my back has been for the past two weeks), proper nutrition becomes even more vital. Fuel your body with what it needs, not what it's craving from lack of sleep.

3. **Supplements.** I'm not usually big on supplements, but I swear by Armaforce. A clinical nutritionist recommended it to me during my advertising days, and it was a godsend when the office was falling sick around me. Make sure to check if it's right for you first.

4. **Movement.** When we're unwell, we tend to curl up and stay still, which can lead to tight pecs, shoulders and upper traps. Gentle movement is great for blood flow and joint health. If you're contagious, stay home, but gentle movement can be beneficial even when you're not at your best. Just remember, it's not the time to push for a personal best.

Stay well, take care of yourselves, and let's support each other through this season. If you have any tips or tricks that work for you, feel free to share!

Stay warm, stay healthy, stay Sassy!

Until next week,
Nikki
