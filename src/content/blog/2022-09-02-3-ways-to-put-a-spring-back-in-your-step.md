Hi Friend,

Spring is here in Sydney — well, technically anyway; the actual weather may say otherwise. (Did you know Australia is one of the few countries that mark seasons by the calendar rather than the solstice? You learn something new every day.)

With spring in tow and my home circumstances changing last week, my schedule and routine have been out of whack. Does anyone else feel frazzled when the regular routine gets interrupted? Here are three ways to help put a spring back in your step.

**1. Accountability buddy.** This can be a person — family, friend, colleague, mentor, or coach — or an app or a calendar you tick off. Being accountable to someone or something gives you drive and structure. Generally we're driven either by proving things to ourselves, or by being accountable to another person. Work out which one works for you.

**2. Take a reset moment.** Re-evaluate what's working and what's hindering your progress. Does something need to be added or removed from your routine for progress to continue? And how can you make that happen?

**3. Break bigger tasks into smaller chunks.** We're in the last quarter of the year — are you on track with what you set out to do? Sometimes we have the best intentions but get overwhelmed by all the ideas (I overwhelm very easily). Chunk it down into monthly, weekly, and daily tasks, then fold that into your accountability — having someone to answer to gives you the urgency to actually get it done.

Go forth and enjoy the spring days and sunshine when it decides to show. If you have other ideas that help you stay on track, I'd love to hear what works for you.

Until next week,
Nikki
