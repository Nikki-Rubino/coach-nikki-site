Hey,

Here's the thing — we're not going to be 100% *100% of the time*. Things happen. Sickness can roll in and slap us right in the face… or in my case this week, the chest.

Out of nowhere, I got hit with whatever lurgy decided to visit. Felt off on Monday, faded Tuesday, and by Wednesday I was awake for all of four hours. Fully KO'd. And it hit me again how much this little biz really does rely on me being up and moving — a humbling reminder.

Was I *meant* to train this week? Yep, I aim to move every week. Did I? Nope. Because sometimes our bodies whisper to us, and other times they whack us over the head when we ignore those whispers. It's OK to take a break. It's OK to back things off. Gentle movement, sure — but if even the bare minimum feels taxing, *listen*. Let your body recover.

Here's the upside: when you've built a healthy lifestyle *before* sickness strikes, you really do bounce back faster. Exercise doesn't just build muscle — it builds *resilience*.

And speaking of resilience — can you believe there are still 13 weeks left of the year? That's plenty of time to start (or keep building) healthy habits before it wraps. Daily walks, extra mobility, lifting heavier, or finally committing to two sessions a week — the small steps now set you up for a stronger, smoother year ahead.

**This week's challenge:** choose *one habit* you want to keep strong or build before the year ends. Write it down. Stick it on the fridge. Check in with yourself each week — progress over perfection, always.

So rest when you need to, move when you can, and remember: consistency beats perfection every single time.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
