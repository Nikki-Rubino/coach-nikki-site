Hey Friend,

Do you ever feel like life is a bit of a hamster wheel — living on auto-pilot? When was the last time you actually tried something new? I get it: life gets busy, and there's always "something" to do.

Have you ever noticed that when you're focused on something, you start to see it everywhere? Selling your house, you suddenly notice all the for-sale signs you pass. Buy a new car, and you spot that model everywhere. Having a connection to a thing brings it to the forefront of your attention.

What does this have to do with journaling, I hear you ask?

**Beginner journaling tip #4:**

> List some ideas of things you haven't done before but would love to try.

Be as minimal or expansive as you like — you may surprise yourself. Then take a look at the list: is there anything you could factor in this week? Maybe a type of class you'd like to try — the first step is simply researching whether any are near you.

Once these things are on your radar, one of two things happens: you'll make the time to get it done, or you'll start to see signs around you that you'd previously missed.

I'd love to hear how the journaling month resonated with you — did you pick up some helpful ideas?
