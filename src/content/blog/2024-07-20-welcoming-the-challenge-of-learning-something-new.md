Hey,

Stumbling across an old video from 2020 sparked this week's topic.

Back in 2020, the world was in lockdown and we couldn't have our usual JB games event. So what did we do instead? We ran a fundraiser for Mental Health Awareness and threw our members and coaches into the literal ring together, livestreamed. Hi — I was one of those coaches. I stumbled across the video while looking for clips for my upcoming webinar, and it reassured me that it's okay to be terrified of something you're not good at.

Today, I want to share some thoughts on something we all experience: the challenge of learning something new, especially when it feels like we're not naturally good at it. This is a journey we should approach with joy, curiosity, and a positive mindset!

I'm currently putting together my first webinar. I'm very grateful I was asked to host it for an educational organisation, but I'm feeling very much out of my depth when it comes to presenting — and it'll be an hour! Another adventure out of my depth is some sales and marketing (blurgh). I love helping people gain strength and confidence, and here I am feeling like a fraud when it comes to being the actual centre of focus! New things are a form of growth, and that can feel really vulnerable, sometimes lonely, and maybe a little scary.

Here are a few tips to help us embrace the learning process, no matter what it is for you:

- **Be patient with yourself.** Rome wasn't built in a day. Every step forward, no matter how small, is progress.
- **Celebrate small wins.** Whether it's understanding a tricky concept, completing a challenging task, or feeling just a bit more confident — high fives all round! 🎉
- **Embrace mistakes.** Mistakes are proof that we're trying. Each one brings us a step closer to mastering something new.
- **Ask for help.** We're a team, and teams support each other. If you're stuck, don't hesitate to reach out.
- **Give yourself space and time.** Learning can be intense, so take breaks, rest, and give yourself the time you need. A fresh mind often leads to fresh ideas.

As we continue to explore and conquer new challenges, let's do so with a sense of joy and curiosity. Learning is an adventure, and every adventure is more enjoyable when we approach it with a positive spirit.

Until next week,
Nikki
