Hey,

As the year wraps up, it feels like everyone is in overdrive — trying to finish all the things, dodging the waves of sickness (hello, lingering COVID), and juggling the festive season's endless to-do lists. It's a lot, isn't it? So, let's take a deep breath together. 🧘‍♀️

It's okay to take a little step back. Whether it's skipping a session to recharge solo, making memories with your loved ones, or just pausing for a guilt-free cup of tea, these moments matter. Your health and sanity matter. And while movement is medicine, rest is too.

Speaking of exciting moments, I had the absolute pleasure of running my first interstate Glow Flow in Gympie, QLD, over the weekend! It was an incredible experience seeing everyone light up (literally) as they moved. The only downside? I lost my voice by Tuesday night. Thank goodness for throat-numbing agents and a microphone during the session on Saturday night! The joy and energy from the weekend definitely made it worth the squeaky aftermath.

I'm also gearing up for a much-needed break. It's been go-go-go since before Bali. Between prepping you ninjas to keep moving while I was away, soaking up long, movement-filled mentorship days (I wouldn't change a thing!), and coming back to life throwing me the curveball of a brand-new studio — let's just say I'm ready for a reset.

I've been making edible Christmas gifts (taste-testing is a job in itself!), planning this year's Christmas Gains video (stay tuned for laughs!), and reflecting on the conversations I've had with you all. A few of you have reached out to congratulate me on the big move. Even if you haven't trained with me in a while, you've shared that these Sunday emails inspire you to keep moving and focus on the small, meaningful things in life. That warms my heart more than you know. 💛

So here's to you and your festive season, however you celebrate. Until then, give yourself grace, hug your people tight, and if you need an excuse to skip the grind, here it is: "Coach Nikki said so." 😉

Wishing you health, happiness, and all the festive feels. Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
