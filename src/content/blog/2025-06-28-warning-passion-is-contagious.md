Hey,

Is there *anything* better than watching someone do what they were born to do?

Last weekend, a bunch of the Dazzle crew went out to see a flamenco performance — and wow. The passion. The power. The precision. You could feel it in the room: this was someone *living* their craft. Years of practice, love and energy poured into every movement. Our star of the show, one of us — Lydie!

There's something wildly inspiring about witnessing that kind of dedication and joy. Whether it's on a stage, in a studio, or out in the world — when someone's in their element, they light up the space around them.

And it's a reminder:

- You have that spark too.
- You deserve to move, create and express in a way that's uniquely *you*.
- It's never too late to explore, try, or return to something you love.

So… what's your version of flamenco? What's the thing that makes you feel strong, connected and alive?

If you're not sure yet — come find it. Come move. Come train. Come play. Because when we give ourselves the chance to explore, *we thrive*.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
