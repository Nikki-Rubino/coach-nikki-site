Hey,

Thanks for your patience — it's been a *full-on* few weeks and something had to give. Between workshops, new faces joining the crew, jetsetters returning from overseas adventures, and a Christmas party to plan… it's been a ride.

Keeping it short and sweet this week, because emotions have been running high. You know that saying:

> Stay genuine. Time always exposes what's real.

Ever have that one person you're *forced* to be around — the energy doesn't match, the values are off, and your nervous system practically growls in protest? That's popped up lately, and it's crept into a few sessions too. As much as I love to say life is sunshine and rainbows, I *am* a Gemini, and when loyalty's involved, that rebellious twin comes out swinging.

But here's the reminder I needed (and maybe you do too):

> Don't let someone else's low vibration pull you out of your peace. Protect your energy like it's gold — because it is.

So instead of letting any negativity hang around, I'm choosing to fill this long weekend with love, gratitude, and the people (and things) that make my heart dance. Your vibe really *does* attract your tribe, and I'm endlessly grateful for the amazing humans I get to see every day at the studio. It's turning into such a safe, strong, and welcoming space for people to drop their guard, move, and connect. Watching this little community grow is pure magic.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
