Hey,

Hands up if you got a little stir-crazy with all that rain lately? Cabin fever *definitely* set in over here. My usual morning ritual of walk-dancing to a sunrise hit pause, and let me tell you — my mood and mental state *noticed*.

But here's the thing: a simple walk is *wildly* underrated.

Yes, there's the fresh air and obvious exercise perks — but that's just the tip of the iceberg. Walking is magic for your **eyesight** too. It gets those peepers adjusting to shapes, horizons, and peripheral views after being glued to screens for way too long (guilty!).

Mentally? Game-changer. Shaking up your environment — whether you're listening to your breath, your fave playlist, or a cheeky audiobook — gives your brain a chance to break from the norm. It's like a little system reset. Even **10 minutes** can feel like a recharge.

And if you're looking to spice up your steps? Try adding a sprinkle of resistance — ankle weights, light dumbbells, a backpack, or a weight vest. Contrary to what you might think, this can actually *help* your joints, not hurt 'em.

Quick fact: hitting 10,000 steps a day takes about 60 minutes total. Out of 16+ hours of being awake? Totally doable. You *don't* need to slog it out in one go — bits and bobs throughout the day count!

**Your action step this week:** pop on your (hopefully barefoot) shoes, chuck your phone on Do Not Disturb, and go for a walk — rain or shine. Try aiming for just 10 minutes outdoors today.

That's it. Notice how you feel after.

*"Almost everything will work again if you unplug it for a few minutes… including you."* — Anne Lamott

Keep moving and stay Sassy,
Nikki
