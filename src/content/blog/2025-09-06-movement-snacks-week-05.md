Hey,

So, here we are at week 05 of Movement Snacks. Which makes me curious… how have you been going with them? Any faves? Anyone tried them all? Or are you sitting there thinking, *"Wait, what snacks?"* (yep, I see you non-clickers).

It's been another full-on week over here, but I often think back to my advertising days when "tired" was just normal. Back then, my days weren't mine, and the only reward was asking myself: *"Why am I even doing this?"* Fast-forward to now, and even though weeks are still massive, the reward is crystal clear.

Last weekend's International Animal Flow Day went off! 19 people from across Sydney plus 2 visitors from Korea, moving together in sync across Pyrmont. And here's the part I love most about Animal Flow: you can connect deeply with another human *without saying a single word*.

Take MinHo, a fellow Regional Leader from Korea. We couldn't hold a two-minute conversation in English/Korean… but on the floor? We moved, we breathed, we synced. And it felt incredible. For someone like me, who *bloody hates* small talk and forced chit-chat, that kind of wordless connection is pure magic.

And speaking of learning, I also dove into the Menopause Matrix workshop: three hours on hormones, sleep, stress, training, and opening up conversations about symptoms that aren't always talked about. Knowledge is power, and having more tools in my pocket to help you is what I'm here for. Stay tuned, because I'll be breaking down the good stuff into digestible, bite-sized chunks for you soon.

Oh and today, a big Happy Father's Day shoutout:

- To the biological dads, the mentors, the father figures in this crew.
- To my own dad, who has spent 42 years taking my random ideas and somehow bringing them to life (those new balance bars in the studio? All him from Eddy's PVC pipe).
- And to those who've lost a papa bear, I know today can hit hard — sending you warmth and a virtual hug through the screen.

Keep moving, keep connecting, and keep learning.

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
