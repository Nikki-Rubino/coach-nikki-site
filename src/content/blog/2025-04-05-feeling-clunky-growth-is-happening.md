Hey,

Have you ever been so focused on something that your brain is literally at capacity? Like, someone asks you what you want for lunch, and you just stare at them because your brain is buffering?

When something is new, it takes up way more mental real estate than when you're cruising on autopilot. Learning = effort. Mastery = ease. And in between? That awkward, sweaty middle phase where everything feels clunky and uncomfortable.

For me, it's presenting. I've done three so far (and counting), but does anything about it feel comfortable? Hell no! Every time, my brain is on high alert, processing a million things at once — what I'm saying, how I'm moving, whether my slides make sense, and if I've accidentally made too much eye contact with one person (oops).

But here's the thing — growth happens in this messy middle. Whether it's learning a new movement, building strength, or nailing a skill, that brain-on-overload feeling is actually a sign of progress. Your brain and body are rewiring, adapting, and getting better.

So, if you're feeling the mental burn while learning something new — whether it's a deadlift, Animal Flow, or public speaking — congrats, you're right on track. Just remember: what feels hard now will one day feel easy (or at least… less like brain gymnastics).

What's something that's stretching your brain right now? I'd love to hear it.

Keep moving and stay Sassy,
Nikki
