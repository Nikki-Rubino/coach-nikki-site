Hey,

Guess what? 🎉 Turning 40 is like getting a superpower upgrade! Remember how in our 20s and 30s, we felt invincible, like we could do cartwheels and backflips without breaking a sweat? (Okay, maybe some of us still can, but let's face it, now we do it with a bit more style and grace!)

Here's the scoop on why training in your 40s and beyond is oh-so-special — and how it can be the most fun you've ever had while working out.

I'm currently in the 4th decade of life, and training has certainly changed. I didn't actually realise it (in my own mind I'm still level 25) until I noticed my younger colleagues' sessions versus mine and a fellow 4th-decade coach when we all train together.

**Strength is the new flexibility.** When we were younger, it was all about wild dance moves and crazy cardio. But now, our bodies are whispering (okay, sometimes shouting) that it's time to focus on strength. Strength training helps keep our bones strong, muscles firm, and metabolism revved up — and lifting weights can make us feel like superheroes. I don't know about you, but I want to keep the capacity for my crazy dance moves!

**Mobility is the magic potion.** We might not be able to do full splits anymore, but we can still rock sitting cross-legged and Animal Flow moves. Mobility exercises keep our joints happy and our movements smooth — basically ensuring we can still play tag with the little humans in our lives or bust a move at the next wedding without feeling like tin robots.

**Priorities get a fun twist.** In our 20s, it was all about looking good for the beach. In our 30s, balancing work, family, and fitness. Now, in our 40s, it's about enjoying the journey and cherishing every moment. Fitness is no longer a chore; it's our playtime, if you make it that way.

**The menopause magic.** Here's a secret superpower: strength training is AMAZING for navigating perimenopause and menopause. It helps balance hormones, boosts mood, and may keep those pesky frozen shoulders (yes, it's a legit symptom) at bay. By embracing strength training, we're not just preparing our bodies for the changes; we're celebrating them.

So, let's grab those dumbbells, stretch out like graceful gazelles, and have a blast! A few simple priorities: prioritise strength and lift things that are heavy for you (a structured program beats doing stuff willy-nilly — at least 2–3 times per week); don't underestimate the power of walking; and find an activity you enjoy that raises your heart rate, so you'll be more inclined to keep it up.

Cheers to being fit, fabulous, and forty-something and beyond!

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
