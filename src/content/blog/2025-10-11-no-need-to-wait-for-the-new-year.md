Hey,

I know the countdown is on — Christmas is *right there* on the horizon. But here's the thing: there are still a good 10 weeks between now and the end of the year. That's plenty of time to build momentum *before* the new year rolls around.

You don't need a fresh calendar to start making change. You just need a few small, consistent actions — now.

Maybe it's:

- Getting more daily steps in
- Upping your water game
- Working towards lifting heavier
- Carving out a few minutes each day for mobility

Whatever it is, starting now — while life is about to get busy — means you're living in alignment with your *why* and your values, not just chasing a burst of January motivation.

So here's your gentle nudge: what can you start implementing today that supports your wellbeing *into* the new year? It doesn't have to be perfect. Just consistent.

You've got time. Let's make it count.

(FYI, want a laugh? I wrote this sitting outside on a Sunday morning, locked out of the house after an amazing sunrise with some whales.)

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
