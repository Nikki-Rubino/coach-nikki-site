Hey,

This week has been a whirlwind — almost back to a full schedule (many ninjas this week inspiring me to book a routine dentist visit, indeed).

As we ramp up to the back end of the year, weekends get full, social activities amp up, and routines go a little askew. This is my 8th year as a full-time coach, and what I've noticed is that holiday time pretty much starts from Melbourne Cup and continues until Australia Day!

Yes, it can be a much more social time, and the to-do list keeps growing. So here's a little reminder this week that YOU time is crucial. Keep the appointment with yourself: to move, to lift something heavy for you, to get inspired by how you want to see out the last two months of 2024.

A video resurfaced for me this week. I know I've written SO many emails and articles about the importance of keeping your muscle capacity and mobility as we age. So this time, I'll just leave a visual front and centre. It starts at any level in life!

Take care and stay Sassy,
Nikki
