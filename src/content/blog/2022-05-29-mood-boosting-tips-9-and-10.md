Hey,

So I'm curious — what was something new you learned last week, or what info did you share with another person?

This week I bring it full circle. Finding what lights you up and recharges you is super beneficial. The next step is to find out who lights you up — who simply makes you feel good, makes you laugh, makes you feel special, or believes in you. Some days we need a third-party perspective to give us a little pep in our step, or to show us something we can't always see for ourselves.

**Mood boosting tip #9 — connect with someone who makes you smile.** You know those people who act as a little ray of sunshine in your life — a friend, a family member, your barista. Someone you walk away from simply feeling better for. Go and have an interaction with them: a call, a text, a random catch-up, a video chat, whatever works for you. Maybe it's your barista or hairdresser — book a service with them, or grab a decaf coffee for the afternoon.

**Mood boosting tip #10 — find your tribe.** We're humans; one of our traits is needing connection and interaction. Did anyone else start to slightly go insane through lockdowns, not having interactions with others? I'm super lucky that I get to work with people I totally classify as my tribe — our gym members are the JB tribe, I have my extended Animal Flow family, and my clients are all people I choose to work with. Your tribe is your crew, the people you connect with over a similar interest. It could be a bunch of mates, your work colleagues, your online gaming crew, your gym or dance class crew. Your tribe is a group of people that make you feel accepted, loved, challenged and thriving. Do you know who your tribe is? If you're in need of one, reach out.

Until next week,
Nikki
