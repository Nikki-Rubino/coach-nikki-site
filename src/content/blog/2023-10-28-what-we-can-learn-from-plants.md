Hey,

Have you ever expected a new plant to just grow, flourish, and become its best plant self overnight? Why, of course not. "Don't be so silly, Nikki," I hear you say. Well, why would you expect that from yourself?

Think about it. We can start training — either with a coach, in a gym, or following a new routine of some sort. We'll do it for a week or two, forget, then come back to it or dismiss it altogether, and wonder why nothing has changed in us. Whether it's muscle growth, weight loss, unlocking a new skill, or cultivating a new habit.

Between caring for a new veggie garden and client conversations this week, there's been some crossover indeed. Plants need so many things to be aligned for them to grow, flourish and withstand the elements. Part of it is their own fighting spirit, but so many external factors are essential too: soil, sunlight, water, food, environment and pruning, as the simple basics. Too little of these and they'll die; too much of something causes issues too. They need all of these, on a daily, weekly, monthly and yearly basis. We can't just plant something and expect it to be bigger, better, or fruit-providing straight away.

Let's bring it back to the human world:

**Soil** — we need good roots and foundations as a base level. Physically and mentally, we need a place to cultivate what we actually want.

**Sunlight** — it's our vitamin D, a mood booster, warmth, nature.

**Water** — we need hydration to stay alive, to keep our tissues and joints pliable.

**Food** — what are you fuelling with? Is it full of nutrients, vitamins and minerals your body can break down and use?

**Environment** — are you feeling supported, challenged, a part of something positive? Do you feel inspired or drained? Is your environment allowing you to thrive, or is it simply mundane and going through the motions?

**Pruning** — plants need to be cut back at times, leaving behind the things draining them. Do you have any soul-sucking habits, people or activities that aren't serving you anymore? It's okay to remove yourself from people, situations and habits that aren't serving you.

These conditions need to be aligned, with small tasks done daily. Those days become weekly habits, those habits turn into monthly routines. Routine creates discipline. That discipline is what continues to help you succeed at whatever you're working towards.

Where are you putting your attention? Is it what you want to cultivate and grow?

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
