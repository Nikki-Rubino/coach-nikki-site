Hey,

Well, there's a lot going on right now. The constant stream of life's uncertainties, external pressures and unexpected challenges can lead to stress and anxiety if we allow them to occupy too much space in our minds. A reminder to simply focus on what you can control and let go of the rest.

A quote resonated with me this week, from Jim Carrey: "The effect you have on others is the most valuable currency there is." Your actions, your words, and the way you carry yourself have a profound impact on the people around you. Every interaction is an opportunity to make a positive change, spread kindness, or offer support. Your kindness, empathy and understanding are powerful tools that can brighten someone's day — and sometimes that's all we need to control.

Fear often leads to overthinking, doubt, and a sense of powerlessness. As a fellow overthinker, afraid of almost everything, I totally resonate. Here are some tips you can introduce into your weeks ahead:

**Set priorities.** Identify what truly matters to you and prioritise those things. This will help you concentrate your energy and attention on the aspects of life where you can make a difference.

**Get your groove on in the present.** Shake off the past and future worries. Dance to the rhythm of the now, soaking in every funky moment.

**Build resilience.** Understand that challenges are part of life. Embrace them as opportunities for growth and learning rather than sources of anxiety. View setbacks as stepping stones, or change the thought to "what can I learn from this?"

**Spread kindness.** Carry the currency of kindness with you everywhere. A simple smile, a friendly word, or a helping hand can go a long way in making someone's day better.

**Connect with loved ones.** Reach out to your friends and family. Share your thoughts and feelings, and offer your support. Connection and love are powerful antidotes to fear and anxiety.

**Let go of the uncontrollable.** Recognise when you're worrying about things beyond your control, and consciously choose to release that burden. It's an act of self-compassion.

**Jam out with self-care.** Grooving starts with self-love. Make sure you crank up your own volume and let your light shine. Fill yourself up first, and let others fall in love with the overflow.

Jim Carrey also said: "You have two choices — love or fear. Choose love and don't let fear turn you against your playful heart." Let this be your guiding mantra as you navigate the week ahead. Embrace your power, focus on what you can control, and choose love over fear. You have the ability to make a positive impact and live with greater joy and purpose.

Wishing you a week filled with love, kindness, and the confidence to conquer the challenges that come your way. Make sure your dance floor is filled with joy!

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
