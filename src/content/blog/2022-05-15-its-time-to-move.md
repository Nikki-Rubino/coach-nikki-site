Hey,

I hope your version of self-care is scheduled for the week! Feel free to share with me, or someone, what it is — accountability helps create consistency.

This week is about an underutilised tool: movement. I'm sure you've seen, or even experienced, a lecture or group session where the speaker gets you up out of your chair and tells you to jump, dance, move around, maybe even sing or chant — Tony Robbins style. There's a science to this. Movement brings on endorphins and feel-good hormones. Presenters know this and are looking to either hype you up at the start, or get you re-engaged for the rest of the presentation.

**Feel good vibes tip #5 — move how you enjoy.** This week I ask: how do you like to move? Do you like lifting weights from A to B, dancing, yoga, walking, swimming, handstands, CrossFit, playing with the kids, hiking, martial arts, tai chi? (In my case, I can't go a day without my Animal Flow practice.) If you enjoy it, you'll be more inclined to keep doing it, and to feel the positive benefits — gaining strength, a skill acquired, a group scenario, aesthetic changes.

**Feel good vibes tip #6 — get into nature.** Go outside! Get away from a screen, breathe fresh air, feel some wind on your skin, take your shoes off and experience some textures. It can be as elaborate as a coastal or bushwalk, or simply getting into your own backyard or out near plants on your balcony. Bonus points if you can mix the movement tip with being outside!

Go forth, head away from a screen, and feel the benefits. Let me know what you get up to.

Until next week,
