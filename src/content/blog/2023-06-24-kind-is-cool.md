Hey,

This week's mantra: be kind. You never know what's going on behind the scenes for someone.

This week, amongst my own close crew, there was anxiety about a surgery, a friend's partner had a heart attack (all is okay), a friend went interstate to visit a dying friend, and I had the anniversary of my big sister's passing. Energy levels were out of whack all around. Hugs were given as all the info unfolded, and acts of kindness appreciated.

Sometimes the simple act of a hug, a check-in, a plate of food, or just a gentle "I'm here for you" smile can help uplift someone's day. Everyone in the above scenarios continued to go about their days to the best of their ability.

Can you check in with anyone who may need it this week?

Until next week, stay Sassy,
Nikki
