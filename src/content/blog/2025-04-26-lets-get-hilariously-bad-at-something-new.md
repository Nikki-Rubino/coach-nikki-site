*"New things are just old things you haven't embarrassed yourself with yet."*

Hey,

When was the last time you tried something new? Something a little out of your comfort zone? Maybe something you *used* to do but haven't touched in ages?

We're pretty wired to play it safe — which, you know, *generally* helps us stay alive — but sometimes, trying something different (whether it's a new gym class, going to an event solo, or tagging along with a friend's random group) can be a tiny act of adventure.

Why am I banging on about this today? Because this week, I threw myself into a *beginner* hip hop dance class... on my own. I'd been toying with the idea of adult dance classes for a while but kept hesitating — until a last-minute decision nabbed me the very last ticket.

Did I nail the dance sequence? Aha, absolutely not. Did I have a ridiculous amount of fun flailing around in a room full of strangers? Absolutely yes.

No one knew me there. I wasn't coaching, teaching, or expected to have it all together. I was just learning. Moving. Laughing. Feeling confused. Driving home with a giant, goofy grin, proud that I let myself feel vulnerable — and just *go*.

Here's the real kicker: I've got some big, scary ideas brewing behind the scenes. And doing little "new and scary" things like this? They *retrain* my brain. They give me real evidence that I *can* handle the unknown — that my fears are just that... stories, not facts.

So the next time you get butterflies about trying something new, just remember: *"If you never try anything new, how will you know what you're hilariously bad at?"*

What's one new, slightly uncomfortable thing you could try this week? Let's get a little bit uncomfortable... and a whole lot proud.

Keep moving and stay Sassy,
Nikki
