This week I'll be sharing one of my favourite ways to incorporate whole-core training. What do I mean by this? A lot of the time, society makes us believe our core is purely the muscles at the front of our stomach — the abs. I'm sure we've all seen the infomercials for the "ab-blaster", "ab-cruncher", "ab-glider", or, since COVID, the "quarantine destroyer" (seriously, it's a thing).

But our core is so much more than the six-pack portrayed in the media. Our core is the stability and strength of our whole torso. It's the bridge that connects the upper body to the lower body. Envision a cylinder with rounded sides, a top and a bottom.

This week I introduce you to the Animal Flow movement called the underswitch. Simply put, it's our transition from beast to crab and crab to beast. It utilises this whole cylinder of core, along with shoulder stability. I'd advise doing some wrist prep before getting onto the floor, from last week's blog.

This week, give the underswitch a whirl.
