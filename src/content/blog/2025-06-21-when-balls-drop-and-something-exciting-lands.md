Hey,

You ever feel like you're juggling flaming swords while balancing on a fitball, all while trying to remember if you defrosted the chicken? Same.

Last week, I had *way* too many balls spinning on the plate (yes, I know that's not the expression… but let's be real, neither was my brain). Tasks were piling up, energy was flatlining, and the Sunday email? That was the ball that dropped.

A few of you even checked in asking if you missed something — bless your organised little hearts. Nope, you didn't miss a thing. I just chose to let that one go… because sometimes you need to. Not everything gets to be priority number one.

And funny thing? The sky didn't fall. Sometimes, dropping a ball is exactly what's needed.

**Why the zebra?**

Zebras are more than their striking stripes. These incredible animals thrive in communities, relying on each other for protection and support. Their tight-knit herds remind us of the power of unity and the strength found in togetherness. Each and every one of them has a unique stripe pattern. Bonus — a group of zebras is called a "dazzle".

Now, back to your regularly scheduled chaos: what's one ball *you* can safely let drop this week?

Stay Sassy,
Nikki

P.S. Yes, I defrosted the chicken. Just in case you were still wondering.
