Hey,

Ever had that one persistent mate? You know the type. Always trying to convince you to do *that thing* you know is good for you… but you just don't *like* it?

Yeah. That's me and running.

Now don't get me wrong — I *can* run. I've done a half marathon before, so I know my legs work just fine. But every time I try to run now, I feel like a thumping elephant. These lil asthmatic lungs much prefer a swim, thanks very much. But as I level up to 42 next week (eek!), I know my bones could use a little more impact than I've been giving them.

My go-to excuse? (oh yes, I have plenty of them) "I don't even *own* trainers — I've been in barefoot shoes for nearly 6 years." Then pickleball came along. I caved. Bought barefoot trainers. And guess who noticed? My runner mate. Excuse: *deleted*.

So a date was set. Early AM. Rainy morning. Centennial Park. No backing out. We toddled. We ran. We caught up. And you know what? It wasn't horrible. In fact, it was kind of great.

Not because I suddenly love running. But because of her. The catch-up. The support. The laughs between gasps. People won't always remember what you *did* for them… but they'll remember how you made them *feel*.

Why accountability buddies are *invaluable*:

- **They show up even when your motivation doesn't.** When your brain offers excuses, they bring coffee and a plan.
- **They gently call your bluff.** "No trainers?" Sorted. "Bad weather?" Bring a rain jacket.
- **They make the hard stuff more human.** Shared struggle turns into shared laughs. It's connection over perfection.
- **They help you remember your *why*.** It's not always about the run, the reps, or the results. It's about feeling good — and feeling *seen*.
- **They get you out of your own head.** Sometimes it takes someone else's belief to remind you what you're capable of.

So here's to the mates who nudge, remind, show up, and run beside us (even when we feel like elephants).

Who is your accountability buddy, and what for?

Stay Sassy,
Nikki
